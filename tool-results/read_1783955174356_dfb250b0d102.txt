   142→      <div class="section-heading premium-heading"><span class="section-eyebrow">Brain Mechanisms</span><h2>Neurobiology of Amphetamines</h2><p class="section-subtitle">How amphetamines reverse neurotransmitter transporters, causing massive release of dopamine, norepinephrine, and serotonin.</p><div class="neural-divider"><div class="neural-dot"></div><div class="neural-line"></div><div class="neural-dot"></div><div class="neural-line"></div><div class="neural-dot"></div></div></div>

   144→        <div class="stimulant-card da"><div class="stimulant-icon">🧬</div><h4 class="stimulant-title">Dopamine Release</h4><p class="stimulant-desc">Amphetamines enter dopamine neurons via DAT and reverse the transporter, causing massive dopamine release into the synapse. This produces intense euphoria and reinforces drug-seeking behavior.</p></div>
   145→        <div class="stimulant-card ne"><div class="stimulant-icon">⚡</div><h4 class="stimulant-title">Norepinephrine Release</h4><p class="stimulant-desc">Reverses norepinephrine transporters (NET), causing sympathetic activation — tachycardia, hypertension, sweating, hyperthermia, and increased alertness.</p></div>
   146→        <div class="stimulant-card mdma"><div class="stimulant-icon">🔮</div><h4 class="stimulant-title">MDMA / Serotonin</h4><p class="stimulant-desc">MDMA (ecstasy) also releases serotonin via SERT reversal, producing emotional warmth, empathy, and sensory enhancement. Post-use serotonin depletion causes depression.</p></div>
   147→        <div class="stimulant-card behavior"><div class="stimulant-icon">🎯</div><h4 class="stimulant-title">Stereotyped Behavior</h4><p class="stimulant-desc">Repetitive, purposeless behaviors (picking, sorting, disassembling) are characteristic of chronic amphetamine use, reflecting dopamine overstimulation in the basal ganglia.</p></div>

   169→        <div class="withdrawal-phase crash"><span class="phase-label">Phase 1</span><h4 class="phase-title">Crash Phase</h4><p class="phase-desc">Immediate aftermath of cessation, lasting hours to days.

## 7. CONSOLIDATED FINDINGS

### 7.1 Blocking issues

1. **Source content materially changed.** The BAC scale (6 ranges in `mg%` → 5 ranges in `mg/dL`, ranges collapsed, symptom text rewritten, "Sobriety" row invented), the withdrawal timeline (hallucinosis and seizures windows narrowed from `12–48h` to `12–24h`/`24–48h`; DT window narrowed from `48–96h` to `48–72h`), and the emergency warning signs (8-of-8 entries rewritten, 3 entries newly introduced) all represent material changes to clinical content that the `alcohol.ts` file header explicitly claims were "preserved faithfully."

2. **Source content lost.** The Jellinek 5 species (Alpha/Beta/Gamma/Delta/Epsilon with descriptions and features — present in source at lines 1277–1340) were omitted entirely. The completion report's justification ("Source HTML did not list all 5 species individually") is factually incorrect. Also lost: CAGE per-question clinical meanings, the Disulfiram 5-step mechanism flow, the Disulfiram-Ethanol Reaction Common/Severe symptom lists, the intoxication "When to Seek Help" sub-panel, and the withdrawal DT emergency callout.

3. **Medication substitution.** Source lists **Carbamazepine** as the 5th anti-craving agent; migration lists **Gabapentin** in its place. These are different drugs with different mechanisms and different evidence bases for alcohol withdrawal. This is a clinical substitution, not a transcription. Disulfiram was also added to the medications array (it occupied its own dedicated source section) — a reorganisation rather than a fabrication, but it does change the medication count and ordering.

4. **Newly-introduced clinical content.** The `emergency.immediateActions` array (5 strings) is entirely new content not present in source.

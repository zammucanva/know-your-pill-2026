# Genetic Syndromes Causing Intellectual Disability

## 1. In one line + overview

**Beyond the general picture of intellectual disability lies a smaller, high-yield group of named genetic syndromes, each with its own physical signs, medical dangers needing scheduled surveillance, and (the modern science this note carries) its own BEHAVIOURAL PHENOTYPE: recognisable personality-and-symptom signatures that convert "an ID child" into "a child with Down syndrome who needs a cardiac echo, a thyroid check, and a teaching style built for his social strengths", the diagnosis tells you what to monitor medically AND how to teach.**

The named-syndrome tier covers a minority of all intellectual disability but a majority of the SEVERE-identified cases, and clinically it pays for its learning in three currencies: the MEDICAL one (Down's heart-and-thyroid; Prader-Willi's obesity machinery; 22q11's calcium-and-psychosis risk; tuberous sclerosis's kidneys-and-seizures; each syndrome's surveillance table prevents deaths), the BEHAVIOURAL one (the phenotype science: the fragile-X boy's social ANXIETY mistaken for autism's indifference; the Williams child's cocktail-party warmth paired with visuospatial blindness; the Angelman child's laughter; the Prader-Willi food obsession needing a locked kitchen, not willpower lectures), and the FAMILIAL one (recurrence numbers that differ by orders of magnitude: Down's 1%-ish versus Angelman's subgroup risks versus the metabolic 25%-tiers; genetic counselling changes next-pregnancy decisions). This note catalogues the syndromes a clinician must recognise on sight (the gestalts and their tells), organises each one's surveillance-and-teaching package, and teaches the behavioural-phenotype frame (the idea that the syndrome's genes shape not only the IQ but the TEMPERAMENT, the anxiety-pattern, the social style, the obsessional tendency) because matching the management to the phenotype is this field's craft.

## 2. Learning objectives

After this note you can:
- Recognise the major syndrome gestalts: Down, fragile X, Prader-Willi, Angelman, Rett, Williams, 22q11, tuberous sclerosis, plus the shorter catalogue (cri-du-chat, Cornelia de Lange, Smith-Magenis, Lesch-Nyhan, PKU, fetal alcohol spectrum)
- Run each major syndrome's MEDICAL surveillance table (the organ checks that prevent deaths and decline)
- Apply the behavioural-phenotype frame: match teaching-and-management style to each signature instead of running one generic programme
- Explain the imprinting pair (Prader-Willi vs Angelman, same 15q neighbourhood, opposite parents, opposite pictures) and why it mesmerises exams
- Deliver the recurrence numbers honestly per syndrome and route the genetic counselling
- Handle the Indian realities: the missing newborn-screening layer, the syndrome-specific support organisations, the surveillance's cost-and-access translation
- Manage the transition dangers: Down's early Alzheimer's window, 22q11's adolescent psychosis risk, Rett's regression-after-progress trap
- Support the family's syndrome-journey: from the karyotype result's delivery to the parents' network

## 4. Epidemiology

**Global.** Down syndrome: the commonest identifiable cause, roughly 1 in 700–1,000 births, risk rising with maternal age (the free-trisomy mechanism); the surviving adult population is growing (the cardiac-surgery era's yield). Fragile X: ~1 in 4,000–7,000 boys (the commonest inherited cause, the family-pattern's carrier mathematics), girls affected in roughly half with milder pictures. Prader-Willi and Angelman: ~1 in 15,000–25,000 each (the imprinting pair's rarity with outsized exam-value). Rett: ~1 in 10,000–15,000 girls. Williams: ~1 in 10,000. 22q11: ~1 in 2,000–4,000 (the commonest of the "rare" deletion syndromes, and the schizophrenia-risk's reason every psychiatrist knows it). Tuberous sclerosis: ~1 in 6,000–10,000.

**India.** No national registry; the hospital-tier estimates mirror global rates on the large birth-base (tens of thousands of Down births yearly; the maternal-age distribution differs: Indian Down mothers skew younger than Western cohorts because births happen earlier, and the absolute population dominates). The detection-and-screening gaps: newborn screening (for congenital hypothyroidism, PKU) still not universal (a few state programmes and the private-tier's availability; the "the child looked normal at birth and nobody tested" pattern); the prenatal-screening layer (the double-marker-and-NT-scan's-and-NIPT's access gradient, urban-private full, rural-PHC minimal); and the syndrome-organisations' uneven geography (Down's parent-networks in metros; the smaller syndromes' families often isolated until an internet group finds them).

## 5. The syndrome map (the aetiology organised as a catalogue)

**Down syndrome (trisomy 21).** The mechanism: an extra chromosome 21 (95% free trisomy from nondisjunction; the translocation tier: the familial form the karyotype must exclude for recurrence-accuracy; the mosaic tier: the milder pictures). The physical gestalt: the upswept palpebral fissures, epicanthic folds, the single palmar crease, the sandal-gap, the hypotonia of infancy, Brushfield spots. The MEDICAL surveillance spine (the table that prevents the deaths-and-decline): the cardiac (the AVSD-and-VSD tier, ~40–50%, the echo in every newborn; the cardiology follow-through), the thyroid (the Hashimoto's-hypothyroidism that arrives silently, the annual TSH; the "the Down child's slowdown" that is often thyroid, not "the syndrome"), the hearing-and-vision (the conductive-loss tier behind "not listening"), the atlanto-axial instability (the neck's C1–C2 gap, the radiograph before Special- Olympics-and-trampoline-style activities, the neck-positioning counselling), the coeliac-and-anaemia screens, the leukaemia's elevated-risk (the treatable-tier awareness), the obesity's-and-sleep-apnoea's adult layer, and the ALZHEIMER'S window: the amyloid-precursor gene sits on chromosome 21, and the brain-pathology accumulates such that most adults with Down show dementia's clinical onset by their 40s–50s, the decline-protocol (the baseline-cognitive-testing at 35–40, the thyroid-and-B12-and-hearing audits before attributing decline, the "is it Alzheimer's or the treatables" discipline, the Dementia notes' audit applied at the earlier clock). The BEHAVIOURAL phenotype (the teaching map): relatively strong SOCIAL skills (the warm, imitative, socially-engaged profile), the visual-over-auditory learning preference (teach through demonstration and pictures), the speech-and-motor slower-channels (the signing-early tier), the "strong-willed-and-stubborn" stereotype's honest handling (the routine-loving, transition-needing profile, the autism-note's prediction-engine management applied lightly), and the relative weakness in abstraction-and-grammar.

**Fragile X syndrome.** The mechanism: the FMR1 gene's CGG-repeat expansion (the full-mutation >200 repeats silences the gene; the fragile-site's name from the old karyotype era; the carriers'-tier mathematics: the premutation-carrier mother's transmission risk and the Sherman-paradox's amplification-across-generations, the genetic-counselling core). The physical gestalt (the post-pubertal fullness): the long face, large ears, the macro-orchidism (post-pubertal, the examination-point), high-arched palate, joint laxity, the flat feet. The cognitive-behavioural signature (the phenotype clinicians must meet): the intellectual disability with the ADHD-overlap in most boys (the impulsivity-attention layer), the AUTISM-adjacent presentation (the hand-flapping, the repetitive tier) with the CRITICAL DIFFERENCE: the fragile-X boy WANTS social contact and is ANXIOUS about it; the gaze-aversion is social anxiety's escape (look-away-to-calm, not indifference), the approach-then-withdraw dance, the shyness-plus-arousal; the sensory over-responsiveness; the anxiety-and-mood layer across life (the social anxiety in school, the later depressive-mood risk); the girls' picture: milder-and-variable (the X-inactivation's lottery; the "shy-bright-anxious" girl-tier, the maths-difficulty-and-anxiety profile, the fragile-X-premutation-carriers' own late-layer (the FXTAS movement-and-cognition syndrome in grandfathers, the three-generation-family's full story). The management's phenotype-matched craft: the social-anxiety-first teaching (the small-stable-groups, the warmed-up-strangers, the NOT-large-noisy-classrooms), the ADHD-treatment's full legitimacy, the autism-adjacent-behaviour's handling without the autism-assumption, and the genetic-counselling's family-wide architecture (the sisters'-carrier-testing question, the maternal-uncle's-diagnosis-at-40-when-the-nephew-presents, the classic index-family finding).

**Prader-Willi syndrome (the paternal 15q11–13 loss).** The imprinting story: the same chromosome-15 neighbourhood carries parent-of-origin-stamped genes; the PATERNAL copies lost/silenced gives Prader-Willi; the MATERNAL gives Angelman. The clinical arc (the two-phase picture every exam loves): PHASE ONE (the neonatal-and-infantile): the marked hypotonia, the poor-suck-and-feeding-failure (the nasogastric era, the failure-to-thrive paradox), the weak cry, the undescended testes in boys. PHASE TWO (from toddlerhood): the HYPERPHAGIA; the appetite-regulation machinery (the hypothalamic ghrelin-and-satiety circuitry) runs without brakes: the relentless food-seeking, the eating-and-obesity era, the short stature, the hypogonadism-and-delayed puberty, the almond eyes-and-narrow-temples-and-down-turned-mouth gestalt, the thick saliva's caries-tier, the sleep-apnoea's-and-scoliosis's-and-type-2-diabetes's obesity-consequences. The behavioural phenotype: the obsessional-food-fixation PLUS the broader obsessional style (the collecting-and-ordering-and-routine-loving profile, the skin-picking's characteristic sign), the temper-meltdowns around food-and-transitions, the ADHD-and-anxiety layers, the relative-social-strength (the talkative-and-pleasant tier). The management's phenotype-matched craft: the locked-kitchen-and-portion-structure architecture (the environmental-management; the food is never freely available; the meals' strict routine; the "willpower lectures don't work because the appetite's brake is missing" family-education), the growth-hormone-treatment's modern standard (the body-composition-and-stature evidence), the exercise-and-swimming-built-in lifestyle, the sleep-apnoea-and-adenoid audit, the behaviour-plan's transition-warnings-and-consistency, the SSRIs'-and-the-severe-meltdown-tier's cautious roles, and the adolescence's "the fridge-lock is the treatment" honest framing. The unmanaged outcome: the obesity-and-its-complications' mortality; the managed outcome: a healthy-weighted, employed-in-structure, family-engaged adult, the architecture decides.

**Angelman syndrome (the maternal 15q loss).** The mirror-picture: severe intellectual disability, the puppet-like ataxic gait-and-arm-posture (the name's old history), the nearly-absent speech (the communication-through-AAC-and-gestures channel), the seizure-disorder's prominence (the EEG's characteristic pattern; the epilepsy's management vigilance), the happy-demeanour-and-frequent-laughter (the excitability-plus-smiling; the "happy puppet" era's retired name, the dignity-framing), the hypermotoric profile, the sleep's short-architecture, the wide-mouth-and-protruding-tongue gestalt, the strabismus. The management spine: the seizure-control with the syndrome-aware-choices, the communication-system-building (the AAC-first discipline), the sleep's-structured management, the developmental-therapy's full-ladder, and the parental-counselling's recurrence-tier honesty (the deletion-form's low-recurrence versus the UBE3A-mutation-and-imprinting-defect forms' real-numbers, the genetic-testing's counselling value).

**Rett syndrome (MECP2, the girls' syndrome).** The arc (the four-stage map: the regression story that must be caught): the early months NORMAL-or-near-normal (the subtle-slowing tier), then STAGE TWO at 6–18 months: the REGRESSION, the purposeful-hand-use LOST (the diagnostic signature), the speech-lost, the hand-wringing-and-washing stereotypies (the hands' constant midline-motion), the decelerated-head-growth (the acquired-microcephaly), the autistic-features'-transient-era (the misdiagnosis-window: many Rett girls carry an "autism" label first); then the STAGE-THREE plateau-and-stabilisation (years of the seizure-epoch's onset, the breathing-irregularities' sighs-and-apnoeas-and-hyperventilation, the scoliosis's-and-gait's-if-ambulant progression, the eye-contact-and-engagement's PARTIAL RETURN, the trapped-in-communication profile: the alert-eyes-with-the-non-speaking-hands-busy-beyond-control picture); the STAGE-FOUR late-motor-decline (the wheelchair-tier's progression). The management spine: the regression-caught-early's genetic-testing-confirmation, the seizures'-vigilant-control, the scoliosis-surveillance-and-orthopaedics, the nutrition-and-GERD-management (the feeding-and-growth's constant layer), the AAC-and-eye-gaze-communication's building (the alert-eyes'-channel, the engagement-capacity that survives), the family's-counselling-and-the-Rett-organisations'-network, the MECP2-genetics' counselling (the X-linked-recurrence-numbers' mostly-sporadic-honesty).

**Williams syndrome (7q11.23 deletion).** The gestalt-and-the-syndrome's-famous-internals: the "elfin" facies (the periorbital-fullness, the stellate-iris-pattern, the wide-mouth-and-small-chin), the supravalvular-aortic-stenosis (the cardiac surveillance's reason), the infantile-hypercalcaemia (the calcium-audit tier), the growth-failure-and-the renal-tier. The behavioural phenotype (the counter-intuitive-profile): the "COCKTAIL-PARTY" sociability, the strikingly-warm-stranger-approach, the socially-fluent-verbal-chatter, the empathy-and-fascination-with-faces (the amygdala's-fear-response-to-strangers running INVERSELY, the social-threat-detector's inverse-polarity): PAIRED with the visuospatial-blindness (the block-design's-and-navigation's severe-weakness, the "can-talk-about-but-cannot-draw-the-bicycle" dissociation), the attention-and-anxiety-and-phobias'-layer (the loud-sound-hyperacusis's characteristic-sensory-sign), the strong-musical-affinity-and-affection-for-music (the islands'-of-strength). The management's phenotype-matched craft: the social-skill's-and-the-STRANGER-DANGER's explicit-teaching (the warm-approach makes the exploitation-risk-education mandatory), the visuospatial-supports-in-teaching (the verbal-channel-first-curriculum), the anxiety-and-hyperacusis's handling, the musical-engagement's deliberate-use, the medical-surveillance's aortic-and-calcium-and-renal spine.

**22q11.2 deletion (DiGeorge/VCFS: the psychiatrist's syndrome).** The multi-system hit: the conotruncal-cardiac-defects, the cleft-palate's-and-velar-insufficiency (the hypernasal-speech), the immune-thymic-deficiency's infection-tier, the hypocalcaemia's seizure-and-tetany-episode (the neonatal-calcium-check), the renal-and-feeding-and-growth layers. The developmental-behavioural signature (the reason the PSYCHIATRIST knows this syndrome): the learning-and-attention difficulties (the 70ish-IQ-band-with-the-ADHD-and-autism-spectrum-overlaps), the anxiety-of-childhood's prominence (the OCD-and-phobic-tier), the adolescent-and-adult transition: THE SCHIZOPHRENIA-RISK; roughly a quarter-to-a-third of adults with 22q11 develop psychosis (the highest-known-single-genetic-risk-factor for schizophrenia, the population's-100-times-elevation; the monitoring-architecture: the subtle-early-signs'-vigilance from adolescence, the thought-disorder-screen's routine, the early-psychosis-route's pre-built-pathway, the family's-honest-counselling-about-the-risk-without-the-terrorising), the bipolar-and-mood-tier's lower-but-real layer. The management's spine: the paediatric-surveillance's cardiac-immune-calcium-palate package, the developmental-and-educational-support's full-ladder, the psychiatric-monitoring's built-in-adolescent-protocol (this population earns the structured-screen, the "the deleted-chromosome's psychiatric-counselling" as standard-of-care).

**Tuberous sclerosis complex (TSC1/TSC2).** The mTOR-pathway's growth-dysregulation: the multi-organ hamartoma machinery. The skin-signs (the diagnostic-doorway): the hypopigmented-maculae (the ash-leaf-spots, the woods-lamp's illumination), the facial-angiofibromas (the butterfly-distribution's reddish-papules, the adolescence's-arrival), the shagreen-patch (the lumbar's-pebbled-plaque), the forehead-plaque, the periungual-fibromas. The neurological core: the SEIZURES, the infantile-spasms' classic-and-urgent presentation (the West-syndrome's-association; the vigabatrin-tier's treatment-window: the spasms-treated-FAST protects the developmental-outcome, the "the-spasms'-weeks-cost-cognitive-years" evidence), the later-focal-and-refractory-tier, the EEG's-and-the-epilepsy's-full-management. The brain's tubers'-burden-and-location predicting the developmental-and-seizure-outcome; the AUTISM-COMORBIDITY'S STRONG-ASSOCIATION (the tuber-count-and-the-early-spasms' links; the TSC-child's developmental-surveillance's autism-screen-built-in). The systemic-surveillance's spine: the renal-angiomyolipomas (the MRI-monitoring; the bleeding-risk; the everolimus-era's mTOR-inhibitor-treatment-option), the cardiac-rhabdomyomas (the infancy's-usually-regressing-tier), the retinal-hamartomas, the pulmonary-LAM (the women's-adult-layer). The TSC-observation: the "-in-one-generation" families'-mosaic-and-mild-tiers'-counselling.

**The shorter catalogue (the exam-tier-and-the-clinical-doorway):**
- **Cri-du-chat (5p deletion)**: the high-pitched-cri's-signature, the microcephaly-and-the-cat-face, the developmental-delay-tier
- **Cornelia de Lange**: the synophrys (the joined-brows), the small-hands-and-the-self-injury-with-the-small-structurally-different-hands, the feeding-and-GI-tier (the reflux's-prominence), the autism-adjacent-profile
- **Smith-Magenis (17p deletion)**: the INVERTED-sleep (the day-night-reversal's melatonin-production-disturbance; the "the-child-who-sleeps-by-day-and-rages-by-night": the melatonin-and-sleep-architecture's therapeutic-core), the self-hug-and-the-onychotillomania's behavioural-signature, the meltdowns'-severity
- **Lesch-Nyhan (HPRT, X-linked boys)**: the self-injury's-harrowing-core (the lip-and-finger-biting, the "the-self-destruction-is-the-disease" teaching; the dental-protection-and-the-anxiety-reduction's management-architecture) plus the gout-and-the-dystonia; the dopamine-pathway's-science-behind-the-self-injury
- **Phenylketonuria (the preventable)**: the untreated-picture (the musty-odour, the eczema, the pigment-dilution, the severe-ID-and-seizures-and-behaviour) versus the SCREENED-and-DIETED-picture (the normal-or-near-normal-development; the phe-restricted-diet's-life-architecture); the MATERNAL-PKU danger (the treated-woman's-high-phe-pregnancy damaging the baby, the pre-conception-diet's- counselling); the newborn-screening's-advocacy (the Indian gap-and-campaign)

## 6. What happens: the three cross-cutting stories

**Story 1: the behavioural phenotype (the science this note carries).** The old model: genes set the IQ; environment set the personality. The syndromes broke the model: each genetic package shapes a PREDICTABLE behavioural-and-temperamental signature, the fragile-X social-anxiety-not-indifference; the Williams stranger-warmth-with-spatial-blindness; the Prader-Willi obsessional-food-architecture; the Angelman laughter-and-motor-excitement; the Smith-Magenis inverted-clock. The clinical translation (the craft): the TEACHING-STYLE and the MANAGEMENT-ARCHITECTURE get matched to the phenotype, the fragile-X child's small-warm-groups; the Williams child's exploitation-proofing; the Prader-Willi child's locked-kitchen; the Rett girl's eye-gaze-channel; the Angelman child's AAC. The generic-ID-programme treats the number; the phenotype-matched-programme treats the child.

**Story 2: the surveillance clock (the medicine this note carries).** Each syndrome carries its own mortality-and-morbidity machinery, and the machinery runs on a CLOCK: the Down heart's echo-in-the-newborn-and-the-thyroid's annual-visit-and-the-algorithm-for-the-40s-decline; the 22q11 child's calcium-in-the-nursery-and-the psychosis-vigilance-in-the-teens; the TSC child's kidneys-every-1–2-years-and-the-spasms'-urgent-window; the Prader-Willi's weight-and-sleep-apnoea arc. The clinical craft: the syndrome-diagnosis's first output is the SURVEILLANCE-CALENDAR (the dated-organ-check-table in the file), because the behavioural-work spans decades, but the surveillance-checks prevent the deaths-and-declines that would end them.

**Story 3: the family's recurrence-arithmetic (the genetics this note carries).** The named-syndrome-diagnosis changes the NEXT-pregnancy's-numbers: Down's free-trisomy's ~1%-recurrence (the translocation-form's-familial-higher-tier, the karyotype's-must-know); the fragile-X-carrier-mathematics (the premutation-mother's-expansion-risk's-50%+; the sisters'-testing; the maternal-uncles'-index-finding); the imprinting-pair's form-dependent-tiers (the PWS-and-AS-subtypes'-from-low-to-25%-plus); the metabolic-25%-tiers (the autosomal-recessive-pattern's one-in-four). The craft: the diagnosis-that-names-the-syndrome unlocks the counselling-that-answers-the-next-child-question, the karyotype-and-genetic-test's justification beyond the intellectual-satisfaction; and the Indian-translation (the prenatal-screening's-and-the-testing's access-gradient honestly mapped).

## 7. Symptoms: the recognition-layer (the bedside signs)

**The behavioural-layer (the phenotype-signature drill):** the SOCIAL-STYLE-axis (the fragile-X anxious-wanting; the Williams warm-everyone; the Down imitative-warm; the Rett alert-trapped; the Angelman excited-laughing); the FOOD-axis (the Prader-Willi relentless; the others' ordinary); the SLEEP-axis (the Smith-Magenis inverted; the Angelman short; the Rett irregular-breathing; the Prader-Willi apnoeic-obesity); the ANXIETY-OBSSESSION-axis (the Prader-Willi obsessional; the Williams phobic-hyperacusic; the fragile-X socially-anxious; the 22q11 the OCD-of-childhood-and-the-psychosis-of-adulthood); the SELF-INJURY-axis (the Lesch-Nyhan's-core; the Cornelia-de-Lange's-tier; the Rett's-hand-mouthing-form; the Smith-Magenis's-nail-and-hand-form); the PSYCHOSIS-RISK-axis (the 22q11's-quarter; the fragile-X's-lower-real-tier; the VCFS-family's-schizophrenia-history-association).

## 8. Diagnosis

**The confirmation-architecture:** the karyotype (the Down-tier's-21-and-the-translocation-form's-finding); the fragile-X's-DNA-analysis (the CGG-repeat's-molecular-testing: the modern-replacement-of-the-old-chromosome-culture); the chromosomal-microarray (the CNV-tier's-workhorse: the 22q11's-and-the-Williams'-and-the-smaller-deletions'); the methylation-and-the-UPD-testing (the imprinting-pair's-resolution); the single-gene-panels (the Rett's-MECP2; the TSC's-TSC1/2; the syndromes'-panels'); the metabolic-screen (the PKU's-and-the-treatables-tier; the plasma-amino-acids-and-the-urine-organic-acids); the targeted-testing's-trigger-discipline (the dysmorphism-plus-the-family-pattern-plus-the-regression-plus-the-specific-signs drive WHICH-test; the test-menu's-cost-tiering for the Indian-pocket). The skin-and-the-eye-and-the-imaging's phenotyping-supports (the woods-lamp's-and-the-retinal-and-the-renal-ultrasound-and-the-brain-MRI's-syndrome-tiers).

**The Indian-testing-reality (the access-layer's honest-mapping):** the karyotype-and-the-fragile-X-testing's-and-the-microarray's availability: the metro-genetic-labs-full-tier; the government-institutions' subsidised-tier; the district's-null-tier (the sample-transport-era's workarounds); the costs' approx-2026: karyotype ₹2,000–5,000; the fragile-X-DNA ₹4,000–10,000; the microarray ₹12,000–25,000; the single-gene ₹3,000–15,000; the counselling's route (the genetic-counselling-centres' thin-map; the clinician's-own-basic-recurrence-numbers' fluency as the fallback).

## 13. FAQ: asked the way families ask it

**"The doctor said it is a syndrome, not just slow learning. Does the name change anything?"** It changes three things. It changes the MEDICAL calendar; a named syndrome carries its own organ-checks (the heart, the thyroid, the kidneys...) which we now schedule; untreated, some of those quietly cause damage. It changes the TEACHING STYLE, each syndrome shapes a recognisable personality-and-learning-profile, and matching our methods to it works better. And it changes the NEXT-PREGNANCY numbers; the name answers "will it repeat" with real figures instead of guesses. The name is not a bigger label; it is a better map.

**"Why is he so different from other children with the same problem?"** Because the syndromes ARE different; the fragile-X boy is anxious-and-wanting-contact, the Williams child runs warm to strangers, the Down child imitates-and-engages, the Prader-Willi child's whole world runs through food. Same broad diagnosis (intellectual disability), different wiring-packages underneath. That is why we keep asking which syndrome: the management-machinery differs.

**"The internet says Down syndrome children get Alzheimer's at 40. Is my son going to forget us?"** The honest version: the brain-changes do accumulate earlier in Down (the 40s-typical-window), AND, the part the internet leaves out, a large share of "decline" episodes in Down adults turn out to be TREATABLE layers: the thyroid quietly failing, the B12 dropping, the hearing muffling, the depression from a life-event. Our protection: a baseline-testing-at-35–40, then every-decline-audited-gate-by-gate before the verdict. When the time comes, if it comes, we will face it with the dementia-team-and-the-tools, not with the panic.

**"She eats everything and steals food. Is she spoiled? We never starved her."** In Prader-Willi, the appetite's brake-mechanism never formed; the brain literally does not receive the "full" signal. The stealing-and-hoarding is the missing-brake's-behaviour, not your parenting-and-not-her-morals. The treatment is architectural: the locked kitchen, the fixed menu, the structured-exercise; the house's design does what her body's-design cannot. Discipline-and-lectures-fail; the lock-works.

**"His uncle is 55 and shaking, and the genetic doctor said it is related to the boy's condition. How?"** Fragile X is a family-story in three generations: your son's full-mutation (the learning-and-social profile), his mother the carrier (the sister-testing-question), and sometimes the grandfather-generation's carriers develop a movement-and-thinking-condition at older ages (the FXTAS-tier). The nephew's-diagnosis often brings the whole-family's-map to the light; that is why we ask about uncles-and-cousins and offer the family-wide-testing-conversation.

**"They said the baby's calcium is low and the heart has a problem, and now at 16 they want psychiatric check-ups every year. Why?"** The 22q11 deletion hits several systems at once (heart, palate, calcium, immunity in infancy) and, in adolescence, it carries the highest known genetic vulnerability to psychosis (roughly a quarter develop it). The annual check does not mean we EXPECT it; it means we WATCH with early-detectors, and the early-detected is the better-treated. Think of it as a cardiac-echo-for-the-mind: surveillance, not a sentence.

**"My daughter with Rett was saying two words, then she lost them and her hands won't stop moving. Did we do something?"** No. Rett's arc is built-in: the development's-reversal-in-the-second-year, the hands' purpose lost to the wringing, the words lost. The crucial-finding-underneath: the girl's-mind-stays-more-present-than-the-hands-suggest, the alert-eyes, the engagement. Our work: the seizure-control, the spine, the nutrition, and the communication-channel built through the eyes-and-technology. The loss was real; the trap is treating her as absent.

**"Is there a cure for her syndrome?"** Not yet for the chromosome-and-gene-tiers: the research-machinery is genuinely-moving (the targeted-trials-in-fragile-X; the mTOR-medicines-in-TSC; the diet-in-PKU-as-the-cure-model-that-already-exists), and the honest-clinical-translation: TODAY's-cure-equivalents are the surveillance-table (the organs-kept-safe), the phenotype-matched-teaching (the capacities-built), and the architecture (the environment-designed-around-the-profile). That package is what the outcome-runs-on, with or without the future's-molecule.

**"Will our next child have the same syndrome?"** Per-syndrome-honesty: Down's-free-trisomy ~1% (the translocation-form-higher, the karyotype's-must-know); the fragile-X-carrier-mathematics (the expansion-risks: the family-testing's and the pre-conception's-counselling); the imprinting-and-metabolic-tiers (some-forms' real-25%-shapes). The answer-is-a-consultation (the genetic-counselling-and-the-prenatal-options-mapped-for-YOUR-family-and-YOUR-access): the number-matters-too-much-for-guessing.

**"The WhatsApp group says a therapy/diet/gene-protocol cured a child like ours."** The groups are your best-practical-allies (the surveillance-tiers'-and-therapists'-and-schemes'-knowledge-outsides-the-clinics), and the miracle-tier-posts are the groups' tax.

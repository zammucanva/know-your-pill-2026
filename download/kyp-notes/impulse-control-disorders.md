# Impulse Control Disorders — The Urge-Relief-Regret Cycle

## 1. In one line + overview

**The impulse-control family shares one engine: a rising internal urge that grows unbearable, an act that discharges it with genuine relief, and the regret that follows, kleptomania (stealing without need or profit), pyromania (fire for the fire's sake), intermittent explosive disorder (aggressive storms out of all proportion), trichotillomania (hair-pulling to visible loss) and skin-picking, a family now organised in DSM-5 beside conduct disorder, with gambling graduated to the addictions (its own note).**

Three clinical realities organise this corner of psychiatry. First, the engine is a LEARNED relief-loop (the brain discovers that a specific act switches off an unbearable urge, and the switch gets stamped in) which is why the treatments that work (habit-reversal training, urge-surfing, some glutamate-and-serotonin pharmacology) target the loop, not "willpower." Second, the shame-secrecy dome over these conditions is the densest in psychiatry: the woman who has pulled her hair for thirty years and never told her husband; the grandmother who steals bangles she can afford; the boy who lights fires and tells nobody; most of this family presents only when caught, or never. Third, the misdiagnosis traffic is heavy in both directions: the impulse-disorder patient accused of criminality (the kleptomaniac treated as a thief, the fire-setter as a delinquent) and the criminal wearing the diagnosis (the shoplifter-for-profit claiming kleptomania, the arsonist-for-revenge claiming pyromania); the family's definitions are built precisely to make that discrimination, and the forensic interface (an Indian reality with real legal stakes) runs through the whole note.

## 2. Learning objectives

After this note you can:
- Define the family's shared engine (urge → discharge → relief → regret) and show how each member instantiates it
- State each disorder's defining gates and its profit-motive exclusion (the criminal-vs-illness discriminator): kleptomania, pyromania, intermittent explosive disorder, trichotillomania, excoriation (skin-picking) disorder
- Run the comorbidity-and-mimic screen: mood disorders, ADHD, OCD-spectrum, substance use, antisocial personality, and the legal-simulation boundary
- Deliver habit-reversal training (awareness training + competing response + social support), the first-line psychological treatment for trichotillomania and skin-picking
- Prescribe the pharmacology honestly: N-acetylcysteine for trichotillomania (the glutamate-tier evidence), SSRI logic across the family, the IED medication tier, and what does not work
- Manage the two forensic interfaces: the caught-kleptomaniac (shop, police, the court report) and the juvenile fire-setter (fire-service partnership, the pyromania vs delinquency line)
- Work Indian realities: family policing vs clinical treatment, the "habit not illness" absorption, the marriage-alliance secrecy pressures, and the service scarcity map

## 4. Epidemiology

**Global.** Intermittent explosive disorder: the commonest of the family (community lifetime estimates in the 4–7% band, young male skew, road-rage-and-domestic-storm presentations). Trichotillomania: roughly 0.5–2% (women over-represented ~9:1 in clinic samples, onset adolescence, chronic-waxing-waning course). Excoriation (skin-picking) disorder: 1–2% (female-skewed likewise). Kleptomania: rare, 0.3–0.6% of the population, but a substantial share of apprehended shoplifters (classic studies ~5% of shoplifting samples meeting criteria; the number to quote in court reports, with its corollary: 95% of shoplifters are NOT kleptomaniacs). Pyromania: very rare, a small minority of arson cases (the great majority of fires set for profit, revenge, vandalism, psychosis, or psychopathy; the "pyromania explains arson" error is the forensic trap).

**India.** No Indian family-specific surveys exist; the clinical reality is secrecy-driven and the numbers arriving anywhere are the caught-and-the-courageous. The Indian tissue: kleptomania reports surface through the forensic-and-liaison routes (the caught department-store patient, often middle-aged-and-wealthy; the "can afford it, still steals" pattern that flags the diagnosis); fire-setting reaches the juvenile-justice-and-forensic channels where the pyromania-line vs delinquency-line decision carries legal weight; trichotillomania and skin-picking reach dermatologists first (the hidden scalp under dupattas, the picked face that fills acne-and-infection clinics for years before psychiatry is involved; the "six-dermatologists-first" pattern is the Indian norm); and IED hides inside "anger problems", drink-temper conflagrations and matrimonial-friction caseloads that nobody routes to psychiatry at all.

## 5. Causes & risk factors

- **Neurobiological**: frontal-limbic control-circuit immaturity or dysfunction (right-inferior-frontal tier, the braking apparatus), serotonin-tone dysregulation (the aggression-impulsivity literature), and the grooming-circuit capture model for trichotillomania/excoriation (normal grooming motor-programs recruited as the urge's discharge-path)
- **Temperamental**: high motor-impulsivity, low frustration-tolerance, sensation-seeking, the ADHD overlap
- **Learning**: the relief-loop's stamp, each successful discharge deepens the track (the habit-circuit learning the family shares with addictions)
- **Comorbid engines**: mood disorders (the kleptomania-and-depression linkage), OCD-spectrum (trich's debated home), substance use (disinhibition co-author)
- **Stress-and-state**: the urge's amplitude tracks stress, boredom, fatigue and understimulation (the pulling-and-picking that live in idle hands, studying, television, phone-scrolling)
- **Developmental**: early-trauma and neglect histories over-represented in the fire-setting and explosive-aggression tiers
- **Indian context**: the joint-family's high-surveillance-plus-low-communication pattern (behaviour policed, feelings unspoken) that drives the conditions underground; the marriage-market secrecy economics; and the absence of child-and-adolescent services that lets the juvenile fire-setter and the pulling teen run unassessed to adulthood

## 6. What happens in the brain: two short stories

**Story one: the switch that learns itself.** An urge rises; a building internal itch with no external cause. The person performs the act (the lift of the bangle, the match, the shout, the pull), and the urge switches OFF, instantly, reliably, with real relief. The brain's learning machinery does what it does with every reliable relief: it stamps the loop deeper. Next time, the urge rises FASTER and the act presents itself EARLIER, not because character decayed, but because the circuit practised. This is why the illness's signature is relief-before-regret: the two feelings come from two different systems, the loop's momentary satisfaction and the observing self's horror at what was just done. And it is why the effective treatments are loop-technologies (competing responses, urge-surfing, stimulus-shaping) rather than moral instruction, which the circuit does not read.

**Story two: the grooming circuit captured.** Hair-pulling and skin-picking are not random acts; they are the brain's normal grooming programs (the touch-and-tend-and-smooth repertoire) captured by the urge-discharge architecture. The tell: the pulling is often gentle, exploratory, pleasurable-in-the-moment, tuned to texture (the coarse hair, the irregular bump sought and harvested), and dissociated-in-the-trance subtype (the hand works while the mind is elsewhere). The captured-grooming model explains the dermatologist-first presentations (real skin-and-hair damage from a normal-appearing program), the boredom-and-idle-hands ecology, and the specific power of habit-reversal's competing-responses (grooming-shaped hand-occupations, the fist, the worry-bead, the knitting) to redirect a program the circuit will run ANYWAY.

## 7. Symptoms

**Intermittent explosive disorder:** disproportionate aggressive outbursts (verbal storms, property-smashing, the physical-assault tier) lasting minutes, exploding from trivial-or-absent triggers, with genuine remorse-and-embarrassment after; no premeditation, no profit, no plan: the storm arrives and passes like weather; between storms, the person may be mild-and-liked (the family's "he becomes somebody else").

**Kleptomania:** recurrent stealing of objects NOT needed, not for value, not for use, the wealthy woman lifting a ₹40 trinket; the mounting urge before, the relief during, the guilt-and-shame after; the hoarded-or-discarded trophies; the shop-avoidance-or-ritual approach patterns; the fear-of-being-caught arousal that itself feeds the loop.

**Pyromania:** deliberate fire-setting on multiple occasions, with arousal-fascination-and-pleasure before-and-during (the drawing to fire, the fire-service call, the watching), tension rising beforehand, relief-and-even-satisfaction after, with NO monetary motive, revenge, ideology, psychosis, or conduct-disorder vandalism as the driver; the interest may include fire-lore, matches-and-accelerant curiosity, the returning-to-watch behaviour.

**Trichotillomania:** recurrent hair-pulling producing visible loss (scalp brows, lashes, pubis, multiple sites and site-migration common), with urges-and-trance patterns (the focused subtype: deliberate, tension-discharging; the automatic subtype: the hand works while attention is elsewhere, the studying-and-television pulling); the swallowed-hair tier (trichobezoar: the Rapunzel syndrome needing surgical awareness when abdominal pain joins the picture).

**Excoriation (skin-picking) disorder:** recurrent picking at skin-and-scabs producing lesions, with the same urge-relief architecture, the face-and-arms-and-bump-seeking, the tools tier (tweezers, nails, pins) and the mirror-rituals; the hiding tier (makeup, bandages, long sleeves, the dupatta-architecture of concealment), infections-and-scarring as the dermatological bill.

## 8. Diagnosis

**The logic.** Each diagnosis is built as an exclusion-anchored definition: the ACT is ordinary (stealing, fire, anger, grooming), but the motive-structure is the disorder's (urge-relief-regret, no profit-revenge-delusion), the distress-and-impairment gates are met, and the better explanations (mania, psychosis, intoxication, antisocial motive, OCD; see the differential) are cleared. The exam-and-clinic discipline: the motive question IS the diagnosis ("what did you do with the bangles?": the hoarded-never-worn answer; "who were you angry at?": nobody, the fire answered the urge).

**Structured assessment:**
- The urge-anatomy: where it rises, how fast, the discharge-moment, the after-state (the four-beat history: urge, act, relief, regret)
- The motive audit: profit? revenge? need? intoxication? — the exclusion set, documented in the words the patient used
- The comorbidity-and-mimic screen: mood (depression's kleptomania-comorbidity), ADHD, OCD-spectrum, substance ledger, personality organisation (the antisocial boundary)
- The damage audit: physical (skin, hair, trichobezoar symptom screen, injuries), legal (caught-before history, pending cases), social (the marriage-and-employment exposures)
- For fire-setting: the structured juvenile-fire-setting assessment frame (fire-interest vs fire-setting history; the curiosity-vs-pathological fascination grading): with the forensic consultation where charges live
- Instruments: named-and-not-reproduced (the Massachusetts General Hairpulling Scale for trich severity-tracking; the NIMH tier for skin-picking): severity trackers, not diagnoses

**Differential table:**

| Mimic | The separating line |
|---|---|
| Antisocial profit-crime (theft for gain, arson for revenge/insurance) | Motive-and-planning present, relief-not-urge architecture, no regret-beyond-caught |
| Conduct disorder / antisocial personality | Patterned transgression across domains, callous-unemotional texture, no urge-relief cycle |
| Mania | Elevated-mood period, grandiosity-and-impulsism global, not act-specific |
| Psychosis / delusion-driven acts | The act commanded-or-justified by delusional content |
| OCD | Obsession-then-compulsion serving anxiety-neutralisation with insight themes; trich's debated boundary resolved pragmatically (treat the loop either way) |
| Intoxication / withdrawal states | Substance timeline maps the acts |
| Ordinary grooming / mild habits | No visible loss, no distress-gates |

## 9. Management

**The shared architecture (the loop-technologies):**
- **Habit-reversal training (HRT), the trich-and-picking first line**: awareness training (the urge-diary, the moment-marking, the video mirror), the competing response (the incompatible hand-action held until the urge crests-and-falls, the fist, the pocket, the worry-bead), relaxation-breathing, and social-support recruitment (the family-as-ally, trained to prompt, never to police)
- **Stimulus architecture**: the environment of the automatic subtype reshaped (gloves-for-studying, the hair-tied-back, the short-nails, the tweezer-purge, the phone-occupied hand), the idle-hands ecology redesigned
- **Urge-surfing and acceptance work**: the urge observed-and-ridden like a wave (it crests and falls in minutes), the de-fearing of the urge itself that lets the HRT window open
- **CBT structure**: the affect-regulation training for the explosive tier (the trigger-mapping, the time-out card, the arousal-Management ladder), the shame-and-cognitions tier for the caught-and-drowning

**Pharmacology, honest tiers:**
- **N-acetylcysteine (NAC)** for trichotillomania (the placebo-controlled-trial tier: meaningful pulling-reduction in adults; the paediatric trial was negative, say both), 1,200–2,400 mg daily, cheap and benign (approx ₹400–900 monthly, 2026 India)
- **SSRIs**: the comorbid-depression-and-anxiety treatment that secondarily thins the family's urges (and the IED-era fluoxetine tiers for impulsive aggression; the strongest medication signal in that corner)
- **Low-dose antipsychotics and augmentation**: the old trich tier (haloperidol-olanzapine augmentation reports, low-quality), rarely first-line now; quetiapine-and-aripiprazole for the arousal-tier where used at all, honestly labelled as low-evidence
- **The IED tier**: SSRIs (fluoxetine the best-studied), plus the mood-stabiliser-and-beta-blocker adjuncts of the aggression literature, with the alcohol-and-substance co-treatment that half the storms ride on
- **Opioid-antagonist tier**: the naltrexone-for-urge reports (self-injury-and-pulling small-tiers), research-edge, occasional clinical trial
- **What does not work**: punishment-and-policing (deepens secrecy, never the loop); disulfiram-for-stealing era folklore; and "explaining the consequences" as a sole treatment

**The forensic interfaces (the Indian reality):**
- **The caught kleptomaniac**: the court-report craft, the diagnostic education of the magistrate (the 5%-of-shoplifters figure, the urge-relief-regret architecture, the treatment-plan evidence) alongside the honesty that kleptomania is NOT a legal defence to theft, but IS a treatment indication the court can sentence toward (the probation-and-treatment disposition)
- **The juvenile fire-setter**: the multi-agency discipline, forensic assessment (curiosity tier vs pathological tier vs conduct-disorder tier), the fire-service education partnership (the structured-and-non-shaming intervention programmes), the family-fireproofing architecture, and the legal course through JJ Act's structures (the note's cross-link to the future forensic notes)
- *India note*: services scarce-and-metro; the HRT architecture is teachable to DMHP counsellors in a workshop (the awareness-and-competing-response core is simple enough; the delivery gap, not the knowledge gap, is the barrier); NAC available OTC-cheap; the family-work first-session is almost always the "from policing to partnership" conversion.

## 10. Indian practice

The Indian presentation-door for each member: trichotillomania through the dermatologist's repeated-and-puzzled corridor (the six-dermatologist pilgrimage before a single psychiatric referral; the note's mission is to convert the FIRST dermatologist into the referrer via the continuing-education tier); skin-picking the same corridor one building over; kleptomania through the shop's-security-office-and-the-magistrate's-court corridor (the middle-class housewife caught in the department store is the classic; the family's first instinct is concealment-and-settlement, and the psychiatric referral happens, if at all, at the second apprehension); pyromania through the juvenile-justice-and-school-corridor (the curious fire-experimentation of boys vs the pathological fascination, the discrimination that decides both disposition and the family's future); IED through the matrimonial-crisis-and-workplace-friction corridor (the "anger problem" that family elders, employers and marriage counsellors all manage-and-fail before anyone screens for the disorder). The family-conversion conversation is the universal first prescription: the behaviour has looked like character (weak, thieving, violent, strange) for years; the medical reframe (an urge-loop that treatment can reach, in which the family's own surveillance has been maintaining the secrecy) moves the household from police-and-shame to prompt-and-support. The marriage-market economics of visible hair-loss-and-skin-lesions add the Indian urgency (the concealing architecture that delays dermatology-and-psychiatry alike, and the clinician's respectful navigation of the disclosure questions).

## 11. Clinical cases

**Case 1: the bangle the family settled for.** A 52-year-old homemaker of a well-off Jaipur family, caught at a department store lifting a ₹60 trinket, the second apprehension (the first settled quietly a year before). Brought weeping with her daughter: "she does not need this, we buy her everything". The history taken alone: three years of an escalating urge-ritual at malls ("the moment before, my chest tight; after, a release like a sneeze"), a wardrobe godown of hoarded-never-worn items, a depression running beneath since a son's migration, and two prior shoplifter-guilt cycles of confession-to-nobody. The architecture: SSRI for the depression-plus-urge tier, the CBT urge-relief work, the family's conversion session (the husband's "but she could have asked me" answered with the illness's grammar; the loop does not read need), and the court-report consultation with the 5%-figure education, probation with treatment. Six months: urge-spikes half-frequency, no new lifts, one relapse warned-and-managed. Teaching points: the afford-it pattern flags the diagnosis; the family's settle-and-conceal instinct is the treatment's first obstacle; the forensic report's honest-but-educational line.

**Case 2: the dupatta decade.** A 24-year-old postgraduate in Lucknow: scalp-patching under a dupatta since Class 10, six dermatologists' corridors, minoxidil-and-steroid lotions faithful-and-futile, the truth of pulling unspoken even to the mother she confides everything else to, the automatic subtype (the hand worked during hostel reading-nights), the focused subtype on the coarse-strand hunts, brows-and-lashes bald through exam seasons. The first psychiatric consultation (referred at last by the sixth dermatologist's brightest intern): the diagnosis named, the two-subtype education, HRT structured (the awareness diary; 70% of pulls had been unconscious; the competing fist-and-worry-bead; the reading-glove; the sister as prompt-ally), NAC added at 1,200 mg, the shame-tier addressed head-on (the "not madness, not character, a captured grooming program" conversation with the mother in the room). Four months: visible regrowth patches, the awareness window widened, one exam-season relapse flagged early by the sister. Teaching points: the dermatology corridor is the referral battlefield; the two subtypes prescribe different techniques; the prompt-ally conversion beats a decade of policing.

## 13. FAQ

- *"My wife steals things she doesn't need, is she a criminal? Should we hide it or report her?"*: Neither: this is an illness with a criminal-shaped symptom, the urge-relief loop of kleptomania, treatable, and known to courts. Reporting-and-concealment both miss the treatment; the correct next call is a psychiatric consultation, and if the law is already involved, a report that educates it.
- *"He is not a thief, he returns half the things!"*: The hoard-and-discard-and-return pattern IS the signature: profit is the thing absent from kleptomania, not presence of wealth; that absence is exactly what separates the illness from theft.
- *"Will she go to jail, doctor?"*: The honest law: kleptomania does not excuse theft legally, but a treated patient with a documented diagnosis, a probation-and-treatment disposition and a recovery record rarely returns to a courtroom; the treatment plan is the best legal strategy that exists.
- *"My daughter pulls her hair, I thought it was a habit she would outgrow."*: It is a disorder of the grooming circuit, with treatments built precisely for it (the awareness-and-competing-response training, plus a simple medicine with real evidence). Outgrowing is the one plan this family reliably does not get.
- *"Is hair-pulling a kind of madness?"*: No: a normal brain program captured by an urge-loop; the visible damage is dermatological, the machinery is habit-circuitry, and the treatment is training-and-medicine, not antipsychotics (the commonest Indian misprescription).
- *"Our son lights small fires, is he going to become a criminal?"*: The discrimination matters more than the fear: curiosity-fire (experiments, education-tier), pathological fascination (arousal, watching, the urge-architecture, the treatment-and-monitor tier), and conduct-pattern (motive-and-vandalism, the juvenile-justice tier). The assessment decides which future, and the earlier it runs, the better all three go.
- *"I have told him a hundred times, control your anger."*: The explosive storms are a control-circuit disorder, not a listening problem; the circuit does not read lectures; it reads training (the trigger-and-time-out architecture) and, where indicated, medicine. Your role changes from lecturer to prompter, a different job, and a workable one.
- *"The picking, is it because of her mental tension?"*: Stress raises the urge's amplitude, but the loop stands in calm weeks too; treating the tension alone (the family's default plan) leaves the picking standing; both need their own treatments.

## 16. Self-test MCQs

**1. The feature that most decisively separates kleptomania from ordinary shoplifting:**
A. Frequency of theft
B. The motive-architecture: mounting urge, relief at the act, regret after; with no need, profit, or use for the items
C. Value of items stolen
D. Gender of the offender
**Answer: B.** The four-beat engine with the profit-exclusion is the definition; everything else (frequency, value) is forensics, not diagnosis.

**2. The first-line specific psychological treatment for trichotillomania:**
A. Psychodynamic exploration of symbolism
B. Habit-reversal training: awareness training, competing response, social support
C. Flooding
D. Punishment contingency
**Answer: B.** The loop-technology built for the captured-grooming circuit; policing-and-symbolism miss the machinery entirely.

**3. The NAC evidence position in trichotillomania, honestly stated:**
A. Two positive RCTs in all ages
B. One positive adult placebo-controlled trial with meaningful reduction; a negative paediatric trial: real-but-imperfect, in a cheap-and-benign medicine
C. No trials exist
D. Works only in skin-picking
**Answer: B.** The honest pair the exam-and-clinic both want stated: adult-yes, children-no.

**4. A 14-year-old sets small fires: fascination-and-arousal present, no revenge-or-profit, emerging before-and-during tension pattern. Among the three discriminated tiers, this is:**
A. Curiosity-fire experimentation: education tier alone
B. Pathological fire-setting consistent with the pyromania pattern, forensic assessment, treatment, fire-service partnership, family fireproofing
C. Conduct-disorder vandalism
D. Psychotic command
**Answer: B.** The urge-architecture present-and-motive absent: the middle tier with the multi-agency disposition.

**5. The legal position of kleptomania in an Indian courtroom, correctly stated:**
A. It is a complete defence to theft
B. It does not excuse the offence, but it is a documented, treatable disorder supporting treatment-oriented dispositions; the expert report's job is education, not exculpation
C. It mandates acquittal on medical grounds
D. It is irrelevant to sentencing
**Answer: B.** The honest forensic line: treatment indication, not defence; the educating report converts the courtroom into the treatment's ally.

**6. The Indian trichotillomania patient's typical route to the correct diagnosis:**
A. Direct psychiatric referral at first visible loss
B. The multi-dermatologist corridor (lotions faithful-and-futile) before a psychiatric referral, often years and six consultations long
C. The court's route
D. The neurologist's route first
**Answer: B.** The dermatology-first pilgrimage: the referral education of dermatologists is the system's highest-yield intervention for this family.

# Child Trauma, Abuse & Neglect

## 1. In one line + overview

**Abuse and neglect are common, under-recognised drivers of a large share of child mental ill-health, and the two clinical acts that matter most are unspectacular: NOTICE (the injuries that don't fit the story, the behaviour that changed, the child who finally speaks) and RESPOND WELL (believe her, write her words down exactly, one skilled interview, report under POCSO, protect first, treat the trauma), because the second abuse many children receive is the system's handling: disbelief, repeated interrogation, and the family's re-traumatising siege.**

The Indian numbers are not subtle: the Ministry of Women and Child Development's landmark 2007 national study found two of every three children reporting physical abuse and over half (53%) reporting one or more forms of sexual abuse, most of it from persons known to the child, home and school leading the locations; NCRB's POCSO-era crime records since (thousands of cases against minors yearly, overwhelmingly known offenders) tell the same story in case-file form. The psychiatric truth is the dose-response: Adverse Childhood Experiences (the ACE pyramid) link abuse, neglect and household dysfunction in stepwise fashion to adult depression, substance use, suicidality, chronic disease and early death; the burden of childhood adversity is the burden under half of adult mental illness. Children do not process trauma as adults do: they speak in regression (bed-wetting returns), in play (the doll scene repeated), in the body (pain, headaches, vaginal/abdominal complaints, the paediatric carousel), in behaviour (the sudden aggression, the frozen obedience), and in words only last, once, quietly; the disclosure is usually a test balloon. The clinical discipline that changes outcomes: recognise the flags, receive the disclosure correctly (the four DON'Ts), let the law's machinery run (POCSO 2012's mandatory reporting; the medical examination; the Child Welfare Committee), and deliver the best-evidenced treatment (trauma-focused CBT with the non-offending caregiver in the room). The child who is believed, protected and treated has a genuinely good long-run trajectory; that is the note's most hopeful and least-known sentence.

## 2. Learning objectives

After this note you can:
- Recognise abuse through its three flag-layers: physical findings (the injury-story mismatch logic), behavioural indicators, and the disclosure pattern itself
- Recite the disclosure-response discipline: believe, record verbatim, no repeated interviewing, no promises of secrecy, report, and explain WHY each rule protects the child and the case
- Apply the Indian legal machinery correctly: POCSO 2012's mandatory reporting (Section 19), the medical examination rules, Child Welfare Committee and CHILDLINE pathways, and the confidentiality conflict's honest handling
- Distinguish the child trauma presentations: PTSD-child-edition (regression, play re-enactment, somatic), developmental/complex trauma, and the abuse-mimicking behavioural labels (the mislabelled aggression and school refusal)
- Explain the ACE dose-response story and its treatment implication (trauma work as prevention of adult illness)
- Deliver TF-CBT's phase logic (safety → processing → integration) and know the caregiver-involvement evidence
- Screen the psychiatric presentations that commonly ride on hidden abuse: the somatic child, the "hypersexual" child, the runaway girl (cross-ref the Conduct note), the treatment-resistant "ADHD"
- Handle the family systems: the non-offending caregiver's support (guilt, disbelief, the family's pressure to recant), and the safety-planning of contact rules

## 4. Epidemiology

**Global.** Population surveys (the CDC-Kaiser ACE study and successors) place any-adversity exposure in Western samples at roughly two-thirds; specific forms: contact sexual abuse in childhood ~8–12% of girls and 3–6% of boys in careful surveys, physical abuse in the high single-digits to teens, neglect the commonest registered form in child-protection systems. The ACE dose-response is the field's anchor finding: each additional adversity step elevates risks of adult depression, suicidality, substance use, heart and metabolic disease in graded fashion (the 4+ ACE tier showing multi-fold elevations). The under-reporting layers: disclosures delayed by years in a majority; retraction under family pressure common; systems' substantiated cases a small fraction of true prevalence.

**India.** The MWCD 2007 national study: two-thirds of children reported physical abuse (beating by parents/teachers the normative experience), 53% reported sexual abuse in some form, with the critical structural finding: known persons dominate, and institutional and workplace settings (child labour) run high. NCRB's POCSO-case statistics since 2012 (tens of thousands of cases annually against children, sexual offences including penetrative assault, with overwhelming known-offender proportions) provide the case-file mirror. The detection architecture is thin: school-teachers carry the main observation load; paediatric OPDs carry the somatic load; CHILDLINE (1098) and the Child Welfare Committees are the protection spine; and the medical examination + POCSO reporting duties bind every clinician in the country. The stigma-complex (family honour, the "what will people say" machinery, the marriage-market calculus) drives both under-reporting and the family's pressure to recant, the second wound's engine.

## 5. Causes & risk factors

(The causes OF abuse, the perpetrator- and system-level map, not the child's)

- Perpetrator profile: overwhelmingly KNOWN persons (family, neighbours, school/workplace contacts; the stranger myth dies in every dataset); opportunity + authority + trust-access is the triad; grooming precedes the event in sexual abuse (the desensitising ladder: gifts, secrets-testing, boundary-erosion; knowing the pattern helps children and parents see it)
- Family risk architecture: parental substance use, mental illness, domestic violence; social isolation; harsh-punishment cultural norm (the corporal-punishment tradition as licence); stress overload (poverty, four children, night-shift work)
- The intergenerational transmission: abused-parents-abusing-children at elevated rates, WITH the honest counter-fact: most abuse survivors do NOT abuse their children (the cycle is probabilistic, not destiny, a sentence protective for the survivor-parent and the pregnant survivor alike)
- Child-level vulnerability markers (responsibility lies NEVER with these): younger age, disability (children with disabilities abused at several-fold rates; the non-speaking autistic child's protection-planning falls to the clinician), social isolation, prior disclosure punished
- System-level: under-supervised institutional settings (hostels, care homes, child labour), weak enforcement of protection law, the family's collusion pressure

**What does NOT cause presentations (the myth-block):** children do not fabricate sexual abuse narratives in any meaningful rate (fabrication is rare and usually adult-instigated in custody disputes; genuine disclosures' errors are detail-drift from repeated BAD interviewing, not invention); "she imagined it from TV" is the defence's sentence, not the evidence's; and abuse does not require penetration or physical injury to be a crime or a trauma.

## 6. What happens in the body/brain

**Story 1: the dose meter (the ACE engine).** The developing brain calibrates to its environment: chronic threat keeps the stress system (HPA axis, amygdala circuitry) on high gain, producing the hypervigilant, startle-ready, dysregulated child; chronic deprivation (neglect) calibrates it to under-stimulation, the flat, delayed language-and-attachment picture of institutional neglect; and both sculpt the developing prefrontal-limbic balance in ways the adult then calls depression, anxiety, ADHD-lookalikes, or "personality". The dose-response is the meter: risk climbs with each stacked adversity (the pyramid's architecture). The treatment implication that makes trauma work PREVENTION: the calibration is partly reversible, early safety plus therapy re-tunes the system; the brain's window for recalibration stays open for years.

**Story 2: the child's filing system (why the symptoms look so odd).** Children file trauma the child way: fragments land in the body (somatisation), in behaviour (re-enactment, the trauma rendered in play and repeated relationship-patterns), in development (regression, the bed-wetting returns), and in speech only if the channel is safe. The dissociation of children is rarely the adult's dramatic presentation: it's the frozen watchfulness, the thousand-yard stare in the classroom, the "daydreaming". The repetitive play (the doll-scene, the repeated drawing) is not memory-compulsion gone pathological, it's the child's processing attempt: a filing system that plays the file till it loses its charge. Trauma therapy for children works WITH the filing system (narrative exposure, the story drawn and told till it is tolerable), not against it.

**Story 3: the disclosure balloon (why testimony behaves as it does).** A child's disclosure is typically: delayed (months-to-years, the grooming's secrecy + the family's silence-culture + no safe adult), partial (the test balloon, a fragment offered to see what happens), retracted under pressure (the family's recant-siege is the strongest retraction force), and detail-drifting ONLY when interviewed badly and repeatedly (the contamination mechanics: leading questions, adult-supplied details, the tenth interviewer's script). The forensic discipline follows exactly: ONE skilled interview, neutral open questions, the child's words recorded verbatim, everyone else (parents, police, doctor) supporting without re-asking. The system's repeated interrogations are the second abuse.

**Story 4: the non-offending caregiver fork.** The single best-researched moderator of the child's long-run outcome is not the abuse's severity: it is the NON-OFFENDING CAREGIVER'S RESPONSE. The believed-and-supported child processes and recovers; the disbelieved child (or the child whose mother chose the abuser; the stay-with-the-stepfather fork) carries a compound trauma: the event plus the betrayal-after. The clinical weight lands there: the mother's own state (fear, economic dependency, her own abuse history) is a treatment target, not a moral test.

## 7. Symptoms: the three flag-layers

**Layer 1: physical/biological flags (the medical route):**
- Physical abuse: injuries inconsistent with the offered story OR with the child's developmental stage (bruising in a baby who doesn't cruise, the non-ambulant infant's any-bruise rule; the TEN-4-type framework's logic: Torso-Ears-Neck in under-4s as high-suspicion regions, named here as a concept); patterned injuries (the belt-line, the implement-mark, grab-pattern bruising); burns with immersion or contact signatures; fractures at unexpected stages; multiple injuries at healing-different ages; the flat-affect child in the emergency room with a serious injury (the pain-indifference of chronicity)
- Sexual abuse: anogenital injuries, pain, itching, bleeding, discharge, STI markers (the examination discipline below); psychosomatic presentations (recurrent abdominal pain, vaginal complaints in the prepubertal girl, urinary symptoms, constipation, headaches) the carousel children
- Neglect: failure to thrive, persistent hunger, poor hygiene, untreated medical conditions, dental neglect, the child chronically in unwashed clothing; the developmental flattening of emotional neglect (language delay, attachment-oddities)
- Fabricated/induced illness (the Munchausen-by-proxy spectrum, the medical-abuse variant): the parent manufacturing or inducing symptoms, the child with the inexplicable recurring "illness" whose parent manages the story and the chart; suspicion arises from the illness-without-pathology + the parent's hospital-affinity pattern

**Layer 2: behavioural/psychological flags:**
- Sudden changes: the aggression spike, the school refusal, the marks collapse, the sleep and eating disruption
- Regression: bed-wetting after dryness, baby-talk, clinging, the developmental rollback of trauma
- Sexualised behaviour beyond developmental expectation (age-inappropriate sexual knowledge and actions (the child who initiates adult-style acts or simulates them in play) a strong flag when persistent; the cautionary pair: normal-development sexual curiosity vs. the scripted pattern)
- PTSD-child-edition: re-experiencing via play re-enactment and drawing, nightmares, night-terrors; avoidance (of persons, places, cues: the uncle's smell, the bathroom); hypervigilance (exaggerated startle, scan-behaviour); the new dissociative flavours (frozen watchfulness, absence-like spells); PTSD's diagnostic structure (criterion clusters, child-version criteria including the play-regression channels)
- Frozen watchfulness and over-compliance (the "so well-behaved" child of chronic threat, compliance as survival)
- The adolescent transformations: running away (FROM, the Conduct note's rule), substance initiation, self-harm (the Youth Suicide note's regulation engine), sudden academic collapse, early/rebellious sexual behaviour
- Emotional neglect's flags: the attachment-difficulty picture, indiscriminate friendliness with strangers (the institutional-neglect signature), the flat affect, language and social delay

**Layer 3: the disclosure pattern itself (the flag most often mishandled):**
- Delayed; partial/test-balloon; single-offered; retracted under family pressure; frequently first-told to a peer or teacher, not a parent; frequently triggered by a safety-education class or another child's case
- The spontaneous disclosure's grammar: often present-tense, often understated ("he does a bad thing"), often vague; the child's vocabulary and fear limits, not deception's marks

## 8. Diagnosis (the response architecture)

**The disclosure-response discipline (the four DON'Ts and the DOs):**
1. DON'T interrogate: the clinical interview is NOT the forensic one: receive the balloon, don't chase the details ("Thank you for telling me. You did the right thing. This is not your fault. I will need to tell someone whose job is to keep children safe"), then refer to the ONE skilled forensic interview (the POCSO-era state systems; the child-friendly studio-interview architecture; the NICHD-protocol-lineage discipline: open prompts, no suggestion, no repetition)
2. DON'T promise secrecy: the promise that cannot be kept destroys the later trust; what CAN be promised: "I believe you; you are not in trouble; this was not your fault; I will tell only the people whose job is to help"
3. DON'T confront the accused or the family arena-style, safety planning and the legal machinery first; the confrontation-siege produces the retraction
4. DON'T re-ask the story at every contact; the verbatim record, made ONCE, is the medical-legal document; every later re-telling contaminates
5. DO record verbatim, in quotes, with time and context; DO report under POCSO s.19 (below); DO arrange the medical examination where sexual abuse is alleged (CrPC s.164A's architecture (examination by a registered medical practitioner within 24 hours of report, without preconditions) the examination is protective and evidentiary, and NO child should be refused care or examination for want of an FIR copy); DO notify CHILDLINE (1098)/the CWC where the home is the unsafe place; DO begin the mental-health assessment with the NON-offending caregiver included

**The Indian legal machinery (the binding layer):**
- **POCSO 2012 (amended): sexual offences against under-18s**, gender-neutral for victim and perpetrator; graded offences; Section 19: MANDATORY reporting by every person (including doctors, teachers, counsellors) who has knowledge or reasonable suspicion of sexual offence against a child; failure to report is itself an offence (with the narrow medical-emergency exception's honest handling); the doctor's examination duties and records; the child's court-protection architecture (special courts, in-camera proceedings, the child's statement recorded at home/safe place where possible, support-person provisions)
- The confidentiality conflict handled honestly: the adolescent survivor who begs "don't tell my parents"; the duty to report sexual offences against minors binds regardless; the clinician's honest move is to say so BEFORE and navigate WITH the child (who is told, in what order, with what protections; the CWC process can substitute for parental notification where the parent is the offender or unsafe)
- **Juvenile Justice Act's care-side machinery**: the Child Welfare Committee (the statutory care-body, orders for care, protection, placement), the District Child Protection Unit, CHILDLINE 1098, and the police's Special Juvenile Police Units
- **Domestic Violence Act / Section 75 JJ Act (cruelty to child), and the IPC's old-guard provisions** where applicable
- Documentation discipline: verbatim quotes, body diagrams, timing, who was present, the child's affect; notes written as if a judge will read them (because one may)

**The mental-health assessment (after safety):** the trauma-symptom map (the child-version PTSD structure; dissociation; regression; somatic), the developmental impact audit (language, attachment, school), the comorbid map (depression, anxiety, the ADHD-lookalike, conduct, self-harm), the non-offending caregiver's state and capacity (the outcome fork), and the child's protective resources (the teacher, the grandmother, the sibling).

## 9. Management

**The frame: SAFETY FIRST, TELL IT ONCE, TREAT THE MEMORY, HOLD THE CAREGIVER. The sequence matters; done out of order, each step re-injures.**

**1, Safety (the phase-zero):** the contact rules negotiated (the accused's access; separation where the accused is household; the CWC placement where home is unsafe, the relative-kinship-care-first principle; the school-staff supervision plan); the follow-up frequency set; the medical needs met (injuries, STI-prophylaxis/pregnancy-prevention protocols where indicated and time-appropriate, the 72–120-hour windows; the emergency-contraception and prophylaxis conversations handled by the paediatric/SART-tier team); and the information-control plan (who tells the school, what, the child's return-to-school plan protects from the stigma-siege).

**2: The legal-machinery navigation (the systems-work):** the report (POCSO s.19, by phone to the police/SJPU or CHILDLINE; the doctor's protection-and-report duties; the FIR and the s.164A examination architecture); the CWC's care-orders where home-protection is needed; the court-preparation work later (the child's testimony-support, the special-court provisions used, the support-person appointed, the case's pace, the years-long Indian criminal timeline and the child's parallel therapy-track being the honest counsel: "the case is the system's job; the healing is ours").

**3, Trauma therapy (the evidence tier):**
- **TF-CBT (trauma-focused CBT), child-and-caregiver edition, the first-line:** the phase architecture, stabilisation/safety-skills (psychoeducation, the feelings-vocabulary, relaxation/coping, the caregiver's parallel work) → gradual processing (the trauma-narrative built at the child's pace: told, drawn, written, walked-through repeatedly till it loses charge, WITH the caregiver hearing it in the later phases: the child's recovery-message "I can tell, and the telling is survivable" + the caregiver's corrective hearing) → integration/closure (meaning, blame-correction, the "not my fault" work; safety-skills for the future; the return-to-normal-development tasks): 12–20 sessions, the caregiver-module evidence making it the child gold standard; the model's adaptation-friendliness in Indian settings (the parent-mediated delivery variants, the NGO-tier training programmes)
- Abuse-specific modules: the body-safety skills (the underwear-rule teaching, the "no-secrets-with-adults" rule, the named trusted-adults), the grooming-deconstruction for older children
- **The dissociation and somatic work:** grounding-skills, the body-symptom's trauma-linking; the play-therapy channel for the pre-school band (the re-enactment's processing in the child's filing system)
- **Caregiver parallel track:** the non-offending parent's own trauma-response (guilt, rage, her own history), the parenting-through-symptoms coaching (the regression handled without punishment; the sexualised behaviour redirected not shamed), the family's recant-pressure countered (the legal-psychosocial briefing: the retraction harms the child and the case)
- Medication: the adjunct-only position (sleep, the severe-flashback window, comorbid depression), no drug treats the trauma itself; prazosin's nightmare-tier evidence is adult-anchored with emerging child data
- The multi-problem family (substance, violence, neglect-braided): the child-protection liaison continuing through therapy; the honest engagement of the family's own treatment needs

**4: The system's dos and don'ts (the re-traumatisation audit):** ONE forensic interview; ONE medical examination (recorded properly, not re-done for institutional convenience); the child NOT displayed to the corridor of officials; the "who this child must repeat the story to" count held as close to one as the law allows; the teacher-briefing sheet (the return-to-school handling: no public sympathy-siege, no interrogation by well-meaning staff); the extended family's siege managed by ONE designated family-communicator (usually the social worker); the media invisible.

**5: Neglect and chronicity (the quieter half):** the poverty-vs-neglect distinction (the malnourished child of the destitute family needs welfare, not criminalisation, the enabling architecture: ration linkage, the Anganwadi, the school-meal, the mother's mental-health treatment); the emotional-neglect's slow work (the dyadic-infant-therapy tier where the attachment wound is early); the institutional-neglect children (adoption-system navigation, the foster-care frontier's Indian reality); and the chronic-punishment norm's shift (the parenting programmes that replace the stick, the Conduct note's behavioural package as abuse-PREVENTION).

**6, The long-run (the survivor's map):** the adolescent survivor's identity work (the self-blame architecture, the intimacy-trust repair); the adult-survivor presentations (the depression, the somatic, the relationship-patterns, the ACE pyramid's downstream clinics; asking "was there anything in your childhood…" belongs in EVERY adult psychiatric history); and the protective truth delivered to every survivor-parent: the cycle is probabilistic, not destiny; the survivor who seeks help breaks it.

*India note (approx 2026): the spine, CHILDLINE 1098 (the free 24×7 referral-and-rescue tier), the CWC/DCPU machinery per district, POCSO special courts (urban-metropolitan and district), one-stop-centres (Sakhi) with the medical-examination-and-counselling bundling, and the NGO tier (the child-rights organisations running case-support and therapy). TF-CBT-trained professionals metro-concentrated (₹800–2,500/session; NGO-tier subsidised); the government psychology-department route for the sustained course. The recurring Indian failure-points are the two sieges: the family's recant-siege and the system's repeat-interview siege; both are countered by documentation discipline and the one-skilled-interview standard.*

## 10. Indian practice

- **The teacher is the system's front line.** In the MWCD study's picture, school and home dominate the abuse locations; the disclosure lands on a teacher more often than on any clinic. The highest-yield capacity you can build: a 2-hour teacher package (the flags, the four DON'Ts and DOs, the 1098 number, the "report, don't investigate" line), the difference between the test-balloon received well and the balloon met with the corridor interrogation.
- **The paediatric carousel hides this daily.** The unexplained recurrent abdominal pain, the vaginal complaint, the constipation, the Indian pattern routes the abuse-child through three paediatricians and two ultrasounds before anyone does the alone-interview. The discipline to institutionalise: the behaviour-flags audit and the one privacy-interview for the recurring-somatic child.
- **POCSO's binding reality for clinicians.** The s.19 duty binds every Indian clinician; the referral-latitude of Western practice (where the clinician may weigh the report) does not exist here; the honest handling: know it, tell the family before they confess-then-retreat, and let the s.164A architecture run (examination without the FIR-precondition, within the 24-hour frame, with the protocol's dignity-standards). The conflict case (the adolescent begging secrecy) is handled by saying the duty aloud and navigating the disclosure-WITH, not by silent breach or silent compliance.
- **The joint family's siege management.** The recant-pressure comes dressed as reconciliation ("it will ruin the family name; she is too young to know what she is saying; think of her future"). The counter-person is ONE senior family-communicator aligned (the grandmother, the trusted uncle) plus the written record's immovability; the recantation's aftermath (the child isolated inside the family) is itself a child-protection concern for the CWC's ongoing watch.
- **The known-offender reality shapes prevention.** The "beware strangers" lecture is folklore's comfort; the honest prevention content is the grooming-pattern teaching (gifts-secrets-escalation), the body-safety rules, and the adults' supervision-architecture of access (the household's one-adult-one-room norms for visitors; the institutional supervision audits). The school's child-protection policy (staff-conduct codes, the reporting-pathway poster) is becoming the CBSE-era norm, support it locally.
- **The male-survivor silence.** Indian male survivors carry the double silence (the masculinity-norm + the homosexuality-confusion myths); their presentations are the conduct-substance channel; the clinic's doorway is the direct-gentle question in the alone-interview. The "will he become an abuser" fear gets the evidence-answer: most survivors never abuse anyone; the cycle is not a sentence.
- **The adolescent's testimony decade.** POCSO trials run years; the child heals on a different clock than the court. The practice architecture: the therapy-track protected from the case-track (the therapist's non-testifying role where the system allows, the support-person's buffering, the school-life continuity, the hearing-preparation when the date comes, honest, procedural, rehearsed).

## 11. Clinical cases (de-identified)

**Case 1: the test balloon handled right.** A 9-year-old girl in a Mysuru school stayed back after class and told her teacher: "uncle does a bad thing when he brings the textbook money." The teacher, trained in a 2-hour package a year before, did everything the note's discipline requires: she did not gasp, interrogate or promise secrecy; she said "thank you for telling me; you did the right thing; it is not your fault; I will tell someone whose job is keeping children safe", informed the principal, and the school called CHILDLINE (1098) rather than summoning the family-assembly. The single forensic interview (at the child-friendly studio-tier facility; open prompts, the child's own pace) elicited the fuller account: the 38-year-old tuition-boarding uncle (a co-resident relative), a grooming arc of gifts and secret-testing over months, contact abuse. The 164A medical examination done within the protocol's dignity-standards (the female examiner, the explained-every-step discipline, the child's mother present); no injuries requiring treatment; prophylaxis protocols reviewed. The girl's mother (27, widowed, economically dependent on the joint household) was received in the parallel track: her own fear and dependency mapped, the CWC engaged, the placement decision landed on the maternal grandmother's home with the accused removed and cases filed; the mother's brother aligned as the family-communicator, and the recant-siege that assembled (two aunts, one phone campaign) was met with the record and the CWC's standing watch. Mental-health work: TF-CBT over 16 sessions, the phases run properly: stabilisation (the feelings-vocabulary, the safe-people map, the mother's parallel module), the trauma-narrative (told-drawn-told; by session 11, "it's just a story I have now"; her words), integration (the not-my-fault work: the blame-architecture rebuilt; the body-safety rules; the return-to-school with the teacher-briefing sheet and no sympathy-siege). At one year: symptoms in full remission, school thriving; the case proceeding (slowly: the honest Indian clock); the mother employed in a tailoring unit; the girl's drawing (brought to the last session): a house, a tree, and the word "safe" under it. Teaching points: (1) the trained teacher received the balloon in the exact four-sentences discipline, the whole case's trajectory turned on that hour; (2) the single-forensic-interview standard kept both the child and the case clean; (3) the non-offending mother's support (not blame) was the outcome's fork; the CWC and the economic plan made the support possible; (4) the recant-siege was managed by ONE communicator and a written record, not by the family's assembly-justice; (5) the therapy's phase-order (safety before narrative before meaning) was the treatment's spine.

**Case 2: the carousel child with the pain nobody scanned.** An 11-year-old Meerut girl presented to child psychiatry from paediatrics after fourteen months of "recurrent abdominal pain with no findings", three ultrasounds, a stool-profile series, two endoscopy referrals (declined, cost), antacids, antispasmodics, a "gastric tonic" era, and a final paediatric referral note: "functional pain? — please evaluate." The psychiatric alone-interview (offered as part of the somatic-child protocol) opened with the neutral channel: the pain-map, school, sleep, fears, and then the balloon: quiet, present-tense, understated: "the pain comes when Papa comes to my bed at night." She looked at the clinician's face to see what would happen. Nothing happened except: "You were very brave to say that. It is not your fault. I will need to tell someone whose job is keeping you safe, and I will stay with you while we do." The disclosure's grammar: two years' duration; the mother's night-shift work pattern (the father's window); the "secret" anchored by threats and gifts; her initial attempt at age 10 (told an aunt, met with "don't say such things about him", the punished first disclosure; the balloon had been flown and shot down once). The response architecture ran: POCSO s.19 report; the single forensic interview (corroborating, with the medical examination per protocol); the mother's parallel track, the hardest work of this case: her initial disbelief-defence ("he is a good father, she watches TV serials and gets ideas") shifting over two sessions (her own marital-history mapping, the night-shift's economics confronted, the placement to the maternal aunt's with contact suspended); the pain's exit: the somatic symptom faded within six weeks of safety (the body filing-system's signature), needing no analgesics; TF-CBT begun (the narrative-work's pace hers), the school-return plan (the aunt-anchored route), and the paediatric file closed with the diagnostic honesty it had lacked: the pain was a message fourteen months in delivery. At eight months: no pain episodes in six months, sleep normal, the therapy in the integration phase. Teaching points: (1) the recurring-somatic child in the Indian system carries the disclosure until someone builds the privacy-interview, the protocol belongs in the differential of every unexplained paediatric complaint; (2) her first balloon at 10 was shot down by an adult; the system's earliest failure was the aunt's dismissal, not the paediatric carousel; (3) the pain resolved with SAFETY alone, before therapy, the message's delivery succeeded; (4) the non-offending mother's disbelief was treated as a state, not a verdict; her economics and marriage-history were the belief's substrate; (5) the verbatim record, made once, held against the father's later bail-period pressure.

## 13. FAQ: asked the way adults around the child ask it

**"Why didn't she tell us earlier? We are her parents."** The delay is the rule, not a suspicious sign: threats, gifts, grooming's secrets, shame, and the testing; she usually DID tell earlier, in a small way, and the reception taught her to stop. Children disclose when a safe adult and a safe moment finally coincide. The question that matters is what happens to the disclosure NOW, not why it was late.

**"She is too young to know what she is saying. Children imagine these things from TV."** Children do not reliably invent sexual detail beyond their developmental knowledge; that is the core forensic finding of this entire field. Fabrication is rare, usually adult-scripted in custody wars, and easily identified by skilled interviewers. What TV gives children is vocabulary, not stories. The default that protects children is: believe first, verify properly, never dismiss.

**"She took back the statement later, doesn't that prove it was false?"** Retraction is the family-pressure signature, not the falsehood signature: the recant-siege (reputation, reconciliation-talks, economic fear) is the strongest documented force on a child survivor. The original disclosure's verbatim record usually outweighs the retraction, in court and in clinic.

**"Will he become an abuser himself when he grows up? I have heard the cycle repeats."** Mostly, no; most boys who were abused never abuse anyone; the cycle is probabilistic, not destiny. What raises the risk: silence, untreated trauma, and shame. What lowers it: being believed, treated, and growing into an adult who has processed the story. Your fear is understandable; the evidence points to help, not to fate.

**"Should we confront him / call the family panchayat?"** Not the corridor, not the assembly, the law. Confrontation-sieges produce retractions, evidence destruction and sometimes flight; POCSO's machinery (the police/SJPU, CHILDLINE, the CWC) is built for exactly this. The family's role is the child's safety and support; the confrontation job belongs to the system.

**"The case will go on for years. Won't the trial destroy her all over again?"** The Indian timeline is honestly long, and the child's healing runs on a separate track: therapy, school, safety. The court has child-protection provisions (in-camera, recorded statements, support persons, special courts) that reduce the load, and the hearing-preparation work (rehearsed, procedural, honest) takes the fear's sting out. Children who are believed and supported navigate the process far better than the folk-tale of the "destroyed by the trial" child suggests; what destroys children is the opposite, the silent, unsupported, disbelieved road.

**"What do we do with the regression; she is wetting the bed at nine years old."** It is the trauma-channel, not naughtiness, and it fades with safety and treatment. Punishment here punishes a symptom of the wound; the handling is calm, private, no-shame, paediatric-consultation for the medical overlap, and patience; it resolves in months in most treated children.

**"She keeps playing it out with dolls / drawing the same scene. Should we stop her?"** No: that is the child's filing system playing the file till it loses charge; the therapist works WITH the re-enactment (the story told at the child's pace). Home's job is warmth and normalcy, not policing the play; the therapy-room is where the processing is structured.

**"Her behaviour has become sexualised, showing and touching. I am horrified."** The sexualised behaviour is a flag and a symptom, not a moral event; it is what the child absorbed rendered back. The handling is calm redirection, teaching privacy-rules without shame, and the therapy's abuse-focused modules. Shaming her for it adds a second wound to the first.

**"We are a decent family. How could this happen inside our house?"** It happens inside houses exactly like yours; the perpetrator profile is the KNOWN, trusted, access-bearing adult; decency does not screen for it, supervision-architecture and openness do. The family's decency is proven by what it does now: believe, protect, and hold the child through the telling.

**Answer: B.** The Indian clinician's binding duty: the Western clinical-discretion model does not apply here.

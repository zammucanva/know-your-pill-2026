# Suicide & Self-Harm in the Elderly

> **If an elder you love has been talking about death, being "finished", or "going away": read section 7 of this note first, then act; call Tele-MANAS 14416 (free, 24×7) or take them to any psychiatrist or physician this week. The talk is usually depression speaking, and depression at this age treats well.**

## 1. In one line + overview

**The elderly attempt suicide less often than the young but DIE of it far more often (planned, lethal, quietly-executed acts driven mostly by untreated depression stacked with pain, bereavement, isolation and the "I am a burden" conviction) which is why the two great life-savers here are unspectacular: TREATING THE DEPRESSION FULLY (this population's most under-delivered medicine, with ECT as its secret weapon) and REBUILDING CONNECTEDNESS, while removing the means from the house and taking the death-talk absolutely seriously.**

The epidemiological signature that defines this topic: geriatric suicides are high-lethality events. An adolescent makes an attempt discovered in time; a 78-year-old plans with weeks of quiet, uses a method that leaves no rescue window, tells no one, and is found by the family who "never suspected". The drivers are unglamorous and overwhelmingly treatable: depression (the single largest factor, present in the large majority of elderly suicides and untreated in most, because "budhapa" and "tension" got the diagnosis), chronic pain and illness (the pain-depression-despair ladder), bereavement (the widowhood's first two years run the risk-peak), social isolation (the migration-empty-nest and the two-person households), financial and functional dependency, and the burden-schema ("they spend on my medicines for nothing"). India's picture makes this urgent: NCRB data have shown Indian elderly suicide rates that are among the highest of any age band in recent years (with the "family problems" and "illness" causes dominating the stated categories); the ageing-plus-erosion of the joint-family's density has left a growing cohort of elders with thin connection-architecture. The clinical message of this whole note: elderly suicide is one of psychiatry's MOST PREVENTABLE deaths, because its main engine (depression) has excellent treatment, its fuel (isolation) has an architecture-fix, and its mechanism (the planned, means-dependent act) responds to means-counselling, but every one of these only works if someone ASKS the elder about death, which is the single act families and physicians most avoid.

## 4. Epidemiology

**Global.** Global and many national datasets show suicide rates RISING with age in men (the elderly male peak is one of epidemiology's most stable findings), with lethality-per-attempt multiples of the young's; elderly attempts are fewer but the attempt-to-death ratio inverts sharply. Subgroup peaks: elderly men, especially widowed/divorced men, especially in the first years after loss; the medically ill and pain-burdened; the socially isolated (rural elderly men in several countries); prior attempters at any age. The Indian angle within the global picture: with life expectancy lengthening and joint-family density thinning, the elderly's share of India's suicide burden has been growing, a demography moving toward the Western risk-architecture.

**India.** NCRB's ADSI recent years report senior-citizen suicides in the thousands annually (a share that has been climbing, with the elderly rate in some reports exceeding the national all-age rate); the stated-cause tables for elderly suicides are dominated by "family problems", "illness" and "financial concerns", the load-layer showing through the trigger-categories, as always. The structural amplifiers: the migration-empty-nest cohort (the two-person and one-person elderly households rising steeply in census-era data), the dependency-and-burden schemas, the untreated-pain reality (the orthopaedic-OPD journey without mood assessment), and the treatment gap of elderly depression itself (NMHS: >85% untreated, the engine left running). The protective erosion: the joint family's historical density (the built-in observation layer) is exactly what modernisation is dissolving, the "near but not with" families see the elder monthly, not hourly; the discovery windows close accordingly.

## 5. Causes & risk factors

**The engine (psychiatric):**
- Depression: present in the large majority; untreated in most (the "budhapa/tension" diagnosis delay); the anhedonia-guilt-burden triad; late-onset depressions carrying the vascular and medical freight
- The anxiety-depression compound; alcohol misuse in elderly men (the quiet-drinker cohort, under-recognised, disinhibiting)
- Emerging cognitive impairment's despair layer (the early-dementia insight window; the awareness of slipping carries its own hopelessness; the post-diagnosis period is a risk-window to be counselled through)

**The fuel (physical):**
- Chronic pain (the arthritis-neuropathy-back triad of Indian geriatrics), cancer diagnoses and their despair-window, stroke and its disability-depression compound, functional loss (the bed-to-chair slide), sensory loss's world-shrinkage
- The medication layer: the sedative-load's disinhibition, the steroid courses, the polypharmacy fog

**The architecture (social):**
- Bereavement: the widower's risk is the famous peak (the man whose household-knowledge was outsourced for 50 years, now alone with the cooking, the medicines and the silence)
- The migration-empty-nest: children abroad/distant-metro; the festival-visits-only contact; the phone-becomes-silent years
- Financial and role dependency: the pension-gap, the property-transferred-and-regretted elders, the status-inversion humiliation
- The burden-schema: self-worth calibrated to contribution; the "eating-and-sitting" shame; the explicit "family would be free without me" conviction

**The mechanism-enablers (access):**
- Medicine stockpiles (the cardiac-and-diabetic-and-sleeping-tablet pharmacy in the cupboard, the elder's own hoarded supply; the "extra strips" bought over years), pesticides in agricultural households, ropes and the isolated-elder's unwitnessed hours
- The planned quiet: geriatric acts are preceded by the affairs-in-order behaviours (the will suddenly sorted, the debts settled, the gold distributed), the final-month signs families recount afterward

## 6. What happens in the body/brain (the mechanism stories)

**Story 1: the burden-arithmetic.** The elderly suicidal state is less the adolescent's hot-storm and more a cold arithmetic: the ledger of "what I cost" (medicines, the family's time, the daughter-in-law's caretaking) versus "what I produce" (nothing, by the elder's own scale; the role-loss has removed every contribution column). Depression runs the arithmetic with its distorted bookkeeping (the costs inflated, the contributions zeroed, the grandchildren's love not counted as production). The treatment insight: the arithmetic must be attacked from both ends, the DEPRESSION's bookkeeping distortion (the medication) AND the LEDGER itself (restored roles and explicit worth-communication: the family's counterspoken statements; "your presence is not a cost; here is what you give that no one else does", plus real role-assignments). The "peaceful going" talk in this population is the arithmetic's soundtrack; it responds to both medicines.

**Story 2: the planned quiet.** Where the young act in storms, the old act in campaigns: weeks of settling (the distribution of possessions, the reconciliation calls, the quiet generosity spurt), the method chosen for certainty (not rescue-window methods), the timing for unwitnessed hours. This is why the assessment question-set differs: not just ideation but PLAN-ness (preparations, timeline-feeling, the "settled" behaviours); why the family's retrospective "he seemed finally at peace" is a red flag INVERTED (the calm-after-decision, the same sinister remission as the young's); and why the means-counselling and the connectedness architecture matter more per-unit here: the campaign's elements (access, solitude, settled-affairs) are each removable.

**Story 3: the physician-miss (the system's wound).** The Indian elder's suicide typically passes through a physician's room in the preceding month, the pain visit, the weakness visit, the sleep-tablet request. The screening that rides along (the PHQ-2-tier plus the death-question asked directly) is the system's cheapest potential save; the "old age, it happens" conversational reflex at that visit is the system's recurring failure. The clinical translation for every physician and psychiatrist treating elders: the mood-and-death screen belongs in the vitals of geriatric practice, with the same routine as the BP check.

## 7. Symptoms: what shows before the quiet

**The speech layer (the family's missed soundtrack):** death-talk in geriatric grammar; "I am finished", "how long will this go on", "God is not calling me", "I have become a sack of expense", "you all will be free"; the nostalgic-undoing statements ("I want to go back to the village and end there"); the explicit-wish minority ("I have the strips saved for the day it gets worse"), the stockpile-disclosure, rare and gold; the "peaceful fasting" talk (the santhara-adjacent presentation; see the FAQ's distinction).

**The behaviour layer:** affairs-in-order (the sudden will, the gold-to-granddaughter, the debts settled); the reconciliation calls (the estranged brother phoned after 20 years); the giving-away; the method-access behaviours (the medicine strips counted and recounted; the pesticide tin moved to the bedroom); the withdrawal-to-stillness (the stopped walk, the stopped temple, the food refusal "not hungry", the depressive engine's body-signs read as "peace"); and the recovered-burden-sign: the elder suddenly "not complaining anymore" after months of pain-talk; the pain did not go; the plan did.

**The situational watches:** the first 6–12 months of widowerhood (the single highest window); the post-cancer-diagnosis weeks; the post-stroke-disability settling; the property-transferred-and-regretted period; the "child visits ended" despair (the last grandchild's departure abroad); and any medical-visit-in-the-past-month with a mood-miss (the physician-opportunity inverted into the physician-wound).

**The assessment screen (the questions, asked directly, this visit):** "In the last two weeks, how has your mood been? Are you sleeping? Have you felt you are a burden? Have you thought about death, that you would be better off dead, or that you want to die? Have you thought about HOW? Have you made any preparations? Have you ever tried before?", plus the family-collateral: "what has he STOPPED doing? Has anything been given away, settled, sorted?"

## 8. Diagnosis (the assessment architecture)

**The formulation specific to elders:** the depression-mapping (the Geriatric Mood note's full ladder, costumes, pseudodementia check, the medical-and-medication audit); the risk-stack scoring (bereavement-window, male-widowed, pain-burden, isolation-architecture, dependency, prior attempt, plan-ness); the means-audit (the medicine-cupboard inventory: done WITH the family, counting strips; the agricultural-household pesticides; the ropes); the protective-inventory (the connection-architecture's actual density, WHO calls, HOW often, WHAT roles remain; the religious-prohibition where present, protective in this population as in the young); and the capacity-and-preference layer (the elder's own wishes about treatment intensity, the honest conversation that the despair-state distorts: decisions made inside depression are the illness's decisions).

**The tiering:**
- **High-acuity**: plan-ness + means + recent settling-behaviours, or the stockpile-disclosure, or a prior attempt → no-alone-hours architecture, means-removal same-day, the treatment-start this week (ECT-tier consideration for the severe), daily-to-alternate-day contact through the first month
- **Moderate**: ideation with the burden-schema, depression-severe, thin-but-present connections → full depression treatment with early-reviews, means-counselling, the connectedness-prescription package, weekly contact
- **The watch-tier**: the recovered-burden-sign, the affairs-ordering without disclosure → the direct screen anyway (the calm-after-decision must be asked about), the family's eyes-open briefing

## 9. Management

**The frame: this is the most preventable suicide population, because the engine treats, the architecture rebuilds, and the means remove. The sequence: screen → treat the engine FULLY → remove the means → rebuild the connections → hold the calendar.**

**1: The engine treatment (the Geriatric Mood note's full ladder, applied without timidity):**
- Full-dose sertraline/escitalopram-class treatment (the "start low, go slow, but GO" law, the eternal-starter-dose has killed elders), the 8–12-week geriatric clock with early-reviews built in (weeks 1, 2, 4, the activation-watch AND the contact-architecture in one)
- **ECT early for the severe band** (the food-refusing wasting elder, the psychotic-guilt states, the treatment-refusing high-risk): in the suicidal geriatric picture, ECT is not the last resort; it is the fastest de-pressor of the risk itself; the family-conversation that overcomes the film-era fear is the life-saving hour
- The co-riders: pain treated properly (the analgesic-optimisation with the physician; uncontrolled pain is a suicide-driver; the "not-narcotics-in-elders" reflex needs the balanced position), the medical-illness management coordinated, the alcohol layer addressed, the benzo-load tapered (the disinhibition-and-fog ledger)
- The treatment-refusal handling: the home-visit option, the physician-bridge (the trusted family doctor administering the first prescription works where the psychiatrist's referral fails), the family-administered architecture (the daughter-in-law's medicine-slot in the daily routine, the adherence engineering of the Geriatric Mood note)

**2: The means-counselling, geriatric edition:**
- The medicine-cupboard inventory WITH the family (counting the strips together, the cardiac, diabetic, sleeping and pain-aid stockpiles; the week-quantities rule applied to the elder's own pharmacy; the cupboard's custody assigned)
- The agricultural-household pesticide audit (the locked-shed discipline, the Youth Suicide note's Sri-Lanka lesson applies identically; the buy-small-locked rule)
- The ropes-and-access review during the high-acuity window (the honest hard layer; the supervision-architecture decision made WITH the family, not by edict)
- The unwitnessed-hours map: the alone-hours inventory (the afternoon 2–6 gap in the two-worker household), and its filling (below)

**3: The connectedness architecture (the real medicine of this population):**
- The fixed-ritual contacts: the daily morning call-tree (the assigned child, the grandchild's good-morning video, the neighbour's knock-and-chai), the Sunday-homework-call role, the walk-cohort's collection duty (two temple-cohort elders assigned to arrive at the door, the Indian behavioural-activation with social enforcement)
- The role-restoration prescriptions (the Geriatric Mood note's struts): the temple-committee accounts, the building-WhatsApp admin, the recipe-consultant contract: RETAINED roles, not token roles; the elder with a job does not do the arithmetic
- The migration-children protocol: the fixed-schedule video-rituals (predictable beats the frequent-but-random), the festival-visit planning with the pre-visit clinic-check, and the son's annual question ("what has Papa stopped doing?") institutionalised
- The institutional tier where home-architecture cannot hold: the day-care centres, the senior-citizens' associations, the aged-care NGOs' visiting programmes, the connectedness-prescription's delivery vehicles

**4: The calendar (the follow-up architecture):** the dated reviews (the first month's weekly contacts, the month-2 and month-3 checks at the treatment-clock's pace); the family's alarm-sign card (the sleep-drop, the stopped-again activities, the "not complaining anymore", the giving-away, each named, each with the call-this-number instruction); and the bereavement-window follow-ups scheduled PROACTIVELY for the widowed elder (the 6-and-12-month checks after the spouse's death, the system reaching toward the highest-risk cohort instead of waiting for it).

**5, The family work:** the burden-schema's explicit counterspeech (the family taught to SAY the worth-statements; specific, not generic: "you are why the cousins still meet" beats "you are not a burden"); the property-and-finances settlement as anxiety-reduction (the transparent pension-and-property documentation; the dependency-fear's antidote is clarity, not more transfers); and the guilt-and-watchfulness balance (the family of a high-risk elder needs the monitoring-architecture WITHOUT the pantheon-of-surveillance; the named-contact rituals beat the camera-in-the-room).

**6, After the fact (the bereaved family):** the elder-suicide survivor family runs the guilt-and-unanswered-questions grief ("why did we not see", "did we make him feel a burden", the property-regret layers), the complicated-grief screen (the Bereavement note's gates apply with the guilt-amplifier), the family's collective narrative-repair work, and the children's own depression-screens (the risk-family's aftercare is prevention).

**7, What does NOT work:** the "respecting his wish" passivity (the wish is depression's output; respecting it means treating it); the religious-faith-reassurance-only route (faith is protective substrate AND insufficient as sole treatment, the temple-visit plus the sertraline, not instead of it); the "move him to the children's city" isolation-transplant (the connection must be built around HIM with his architecture, or the move adds loss to illness); and the sedative-solution (the night-tablet deepen-the-fog approach, the disinhibition-and-falls ledger again).

*India note (approx 2026): the crisis spine, Tele-MANAS 14416 (the elder's family calls; the elder himself rarely will; the family-call architecture matters), the district-geriatric and psychiatry OPDs, the ECT courses at government tier (₹100–500/session public; ₹2,000–5,000 private), the NGO senior-care tier (day-care, elder-helplines, the visiting-companion programmes in metros). The single cheapest Indian intervention: the physician-OPD's two-question screen riding the vitals; every elder with pain, weakness or a sleep-complaint gets the mood-and-death questions; the system's catch-rate would multiply overnight.*

## 10. Indian practice

- **The "budhapa" screen-block is the kill-chain's first link.** The Indian elder's death-talk, food-refusal and stopped-activities pass through the family's normalisation for months ("old people talk like this"), the screen that breaks the chain is the family's ONE reframed question: "is it old age, or is it depression wearing old age's clothes?": the question this note exists to put into circulation.
- **The widower's window deserves proactive systems.** The Indian widower's first year (the outsourced-household exposure, the silence-architecture, the sons' assumption that "father is strong") is the classic risk-peak; the practical outreach: the 6-and-12-month post-bereavement checks scheduled AT the spouse's death registration point (the system's one touchpoint), the cooking-and-medicines scaffolding assigned in the family meeting, and the walk-cohort's collection-duty engineered before the hundredth day.
- **The santhara/sallekhana interface needs clinical clarity.** Where a lifelong Jain or devout elder discusses a religious fast-to-death: the distinction is between the community-witnessed, tradition-framed practice of an able-bodied elder at peace AND depression's decision wearing the idiom (the bed-bound, pain-burdened, burden-scheming elder "choosing to stop being trouble"). The clinical frame: treat the depression first, if the wish persists in a treated, unburdened, community-supported elder, the practice framework belongs to the family and its tradition, not to the clinic's veto. But depression-found-means-fast-talk gets the treatment-first position every time. (This is a delicate, litigation-adjacent zone: document the capacity-and-mood assessment carefully.)
- **The property-regret cluster.** The transferred-the-property-and-now-dependent elder ("we gave everything to the sons") carries the humiliation-and-fear layer with the dependency, the preventive practice (the Dementia-Management note's advance-planning floor): transparent retained-control structures (the lifetime-interest documents, the pension-and-expenses clarity) discussed BEFORE the transfer, not litigated after the despair.
- **The physician-opportunity must be taught, not assumed.** Indian elders see physicians monthly (the BP, the diabetes, the joints) and the mood-screen rides the vitals only where the physician has been trained to include it, the two-question addition (the "stopped doing?" and the direct death-question) is the CME module with the highest life-yield in geriatric practice.
- **The festival-visit screen.** The NRI/distant children's annual visits are the observation-windows: the son's walk-through-the-house assessment (the fridge's emptiness, the medicine-cupboard's chaos or hoarding, the stopped-walk, the father's weight), the family-debrief question ("what changed since last year?") converts the visit into the screen.
- **The aftercare of the attempt-elder's family.** Post-attempt elders return home to families oscillating between vigilance-siege and resentment-silence; the clinical repair: the alarm-sign card (named signs, one number), the assigned-contact rituals (not the camera), and the family's own guilt-grief work begun in the first month; the second-attempt window is guarded by the family's structured eyes, not its raw fear.

## 11. Clinical cases (de-identified)

**Case 1: the widower's hundredth day.** A 71-year-old Patiala farmer was brought by his son after the son's wife found him "counting the tractor's pesticide tins at 5 a.m.": the disclosure that cracked the three-month quiet the family had read as "father is finally at peace since mother died". The assembled history: the wife's death 14 months prior; the first months of loud grief normalising into a settled stillness the family celebrated ("he has accepted"); the cooking outsourced to the daughter-in-law; the stopped evening temple; the 5 kg loss; the night-waking ("I sit; what is there to sleep for"); the affair-settling (the land-records sorted, the gold given to the granddaughter "before it causes fights"); the death-grammar heard by the grandson and reported upward ("Dadaji says he has stayed too long and the field deserves a better keeper"). The screen, asked directly: yes; the burden-conviction ("I eat their food and give nothing"); yes: the considered ways (the pesticide tins moved to the bedroom side, the stockpile found); no prior attempt; the plan-ness stage: "waiting for it to get worse". Formulation: severe geriatric depression (the pseudodementia-check clean; the medical audit: arthritis-pain undertreated, B12 borderline, hearing loss unaided) with the burden-schema, the widower-window, the means-access and the affairs-settling, high-acuity. Management: the same-day means-removal (the tins locked at the neighbour's with the SARDAR-register noted; the week-quantities rule for the medicines; the afternoon-alone-hours map filled by the grandson's post-school homework-at-Dadaji's-table assignment, the role and the supervision in one instrument); the engine-treatment begun without timidity (sertraline titrated to full dose with the early-review architecture; the arthritis-pain optimised with the physician; the hearing-aid route; the B12 course; the family ECT-briefing held ready in the file in case the food-refusal deepened; it did not); the connectedness rebuild (the temple-cohort's two elders assigned the morning-collection duty; the son's fixed evening call; the field-work role-retention: supervision-not-substitution, the farmer keeps the decisions and the accounts, the son does the lifting); the counterspeech taught explicitly (the son's scripted statement at the family dinner: "the field is yours while you are; we work FOR you", specific, not generic). At 3 months: PHQ-tier halved, weight returning, the temple evenings back; at 8 months: full remission, the tins re-locked without drama, the grandson's homework-table now the house's fixed furniture. The son's file-entry (verbatim, for teaching): "we thought the quiet meant peace. It meant he had decided." Teaching points: (1) the "finally at peace" misread of the widower's quiet, the calm-after-decision presenting as acceptance; (2) the grandson's upward report was the screen's delivery-vehicle (children hear the grammar adults dismiss); (3) the pesticide-tins-at-5-a.m. was the stockpile-disclosure, the highest-acuity sign in the system; (4) the homework-table instrument: one piece of furniture delivered the supervision, the role AND the connection; (5) the means-lock at the neighbour's with documentation, the Indian rural execution of the Sri-Lanka lesson.

**Case 2: the cancer-diagnosis month.** A 68-year-old Chennai retired teacher was referred by the oncology team after a palliative-consultation disclosure: "I have the strips saved for when the treatment gets bad." Newly diagnosed stage-III colonic disease, surgery scheduled; the oncology fellow (trained in a liaison-module) asked the wish-questions and got the full grammar: the fear of being "the family's expensive burden" (the sold-land-for-treatment economics of the middle-class Indian cancer family), the "I have taught my last class; what remains", the insomnia and the 4-a.m. arithmetic; no prior psychiatric history; the family (the daughter, a nurse) oscillating between the "Be positive, Aunty-attitude" cheerfulness regime and their own panic. Assessment: depressive disorder of the cancer-window (the wish-for-hastened-death tracked the mood-and-pain far more than the disease-stage, the palliative literature's key finding applied); the means-audit: the saved strip-stockpile (her own diabetic-and-sleeping medications, counted); the protective-layer: the granddaughter's board year ("I want to see her result: I think I want that"). Management: the engine-treatment begun the same week (sertraline titrated; the sleep-architecture restored first with the CBT-I-lite protocol, the 4-a.m. arithmetic's fuel); the means-removed with her consent (the strips surrendered to the nurse-daughter's custody with the week-quantities rule, consented, documented, dignity intact); the economics-despair addressed concretely (the insurance-PMJAY-and-scheme mapping with the hospital's social-work team; the "expensive burden" arithmetic corrected with the actual numbers, not reassurances); the role-prescription (the granddaughter's board-year project assigned: the daily-phone quiz with "Patti's explanations", the teacher-role restored in cancer's window); the oncology-liaison held (the pain-plan coordinated; the treatment-competence supported: the surgery went ahead with her consent intact and calm). At 4 months: post-surgery, adjuvant therapy tolerated, the wish-for-hastened-death gone ("I ask myself why I said those things; it was the nights"), the granddaughter's board year with Patti's quiz-table intact. Teaching points: (1) the wish-for-hastened-death in the cancer-window tracks depression-and-pain, not staging; treat both and the wish softens (the palliative finding that must enter every oncology corridor); (2) the fellow's training (the liaison-module's two questions) was the catch-point, the physician-opportunity in action; (3) the stockpile was her OWN hoarded pharmacy, the elder's means-audit includes the medicine-cupboard she manages; (4) the "Be positive" family regime deepens the silence, the counterspeech-teaching replaced it; (5) the economics-despair got numbers-and-schemes, not words, the burden-arithmetic fought with arithmetic.

## 13. FAQ: asked the way families ask it

**"He talks about death all the time. Isn't that just what old people do?"** Old people talk about death; depressed old people talk about being FINISHED, being a burden, and God not calling. The way to tell the difference is not guessing, it is asking: "do you ever feel you would be better off dead, or that we would be better off without you?" The question does not hurt him; the question is the screen. And the answer, once heard, gets him the treatment that makes this the most preventable of all suicides.

**"She wants to stop eating and 'go peacefully'. She says it is her santhara. Do we respect it?"** The tradition of a lifelong practice, community-witnessed, in an elder who is at peace and not ill-in-mood; that is one thing, and rare. Far more often, a "peaceful fast" in a frail, pain-burdened elder who has been talking burden-talk IS depression wearing the idiom of faith. Our position: treat the depression first, fully, and then see what remains of the wish; in most cases the wish was the illness's, and it dissolves with the nights restored and the burden-arithmetic corrected. Where a genuine, settled, tradition-framed wish persists in a treated and unburdened elder, the family and its religious community hold that framework, but the clinic's file must first document the mood-and-capacity assessment.

**"We asked him and he denied everything. Can we relax?"** The direct screen is the necessary step, not the sufficient one; the elder's denial to protect the family is real. The corroborating layer: the stopped-activities question ("what has he stopped?"), the affairs-ordering signs, the giving-away, the "not complaining anymore", the medicine-cupboard's stockpile. And the true answer to "can we relax" is: the screen repeats; the risk is a state, and states change; the alarm-sign card in the family's hand is the ongoing screen.

**"Won't the medicines take weeks? He is like this NOW."** The honest clock: the full antidepressant effect takes weeks, but the risk-architecture does not: the means-lock happens today, the connectedness-rituals start tomorrow, the family's counterspeech starts tonight, and where the picture is severe (the refusing-food, the wasting, the intense plan-ness) ECT acts within days and is the safest fast instrument we have for exactly this. The weeks of waiting are held by the architecture, not by hoping.

**"Is he not just choosing rationally? He is ill and dependent."** Track where the "choice" is coming from: in the untreated-depression state, the ledger itself is distorted, costs inflated, contributions zeroed, the grandchildren's love not counted. Most elders who wanted to die inside depression, once treated, are glad to be alive; that is the follow-up finding, and the reason "respecting the wish" cannot mean "respecting the depression's arithmetic". The wish deserves treatment before it deserves compliance.

**"What do we actually remove from the house?"** The full pharmacy to week-quantities (counted together, custody assigned, her own saved strips included), the pesticide shed locked with buy-small-locked rules in agricultural homes, the rope-and-access review during the danger weeks, and the alcohol audit where he drinks. Friction at the campaign's entrance: the planned quiet needs access and solitude; your job is to make both slow.

**"Should someone stay with him around the clock?"** In the highest-acuity days: supervised hours, yes, but round-the-clock surveillance decays in a week and humiliates in a month. The sustainable architecture is the ritual one: the fixed contacts that fill the alone-hours (the grandson's homework table, the walk-cohort's collection, the evening call), the alarm-sign card, and the dated reviews. Eyes that are structured beat eyes that are exhausted.

**"He refuses to see a psychiatrist. He says only mad people go."** Bring the treatment to the door: the trusted family physician prescribes the first course (with psychiatric tele-consult behind him; Tele-MANAS 14416 and the video-opd tier), the frame is "the sleep-and-appetite medicine for your weakness", the family-administered medicine-slot runs in the daily routine, and the psychiatrist enters the story later, once the fog has lifted enough for the frame to matter less. The illness's pride-defence is treated around, not argued with.

**"She attempted and survived. Will she try again?"** The prior attempt is the strongest predictor, and the highest-risk window is the weeks right after: the follow-up architecture (the weekly contacts, the means-counsel, the family's structured eyes, the treatment held at full dose) is precisely what bends that curve. The families that hold the calendar do better than the families that hold their breath.

**"After he died, we found he had sorted everything; the will, the gold. Did he plan it right under our eyes?"** Yes, that is the geriatric act's shape: the campaign's quiet weeks, the settling-behaviours mistaken for acceptance. The answer to the family's guilt is not blame (his illness hid it from loving people) but conversion: the next widower in the family's circle gets the 6-month check, the next "peaceful quiet" gets the screen, and the grandson's upward-reporting channel gets honoured. The grief-work and the prevention-work are the same work here.

## 14. Exam & student lens

**The skeleton for viva:** the lethality inversion (fewer attempts, higher completion; planned, lethal, unwitnessed) → the risk-stack (depression untreated > pain/illness > bereavement-window (widower!) > isolation-architecture > dependency-burden-schema > access) → the screen (direct questions + the stopped-activities collateral) → the quartet (engine-treatment fully incl. ECT-early, means-removal, connectedness-prescriptions, the calendar).

**Mnemonics:** the risk-stack, "D-PBI-BA" (Depression, Pain/Bereavement, Isolation, Burden-arithmetic, Access); the campaign's signs, "the SETTLED elder": Sorted affairs, Emotional calm-after-decision, Told-in-grammar, Lost-weight, Extra strips-stockpiled, Death-rituals planned.

**High-yield facts:**
- Elderly attempt-to-death ratio inverts: attempts fewer, completions multiples of the young's
- The highest subgroup: elderly WIDOWED MEN in the first 1–2 years post-loss
- NCRB: Indian senior-citizen suicides in the thousands/year, share climbing; stated-causes dominated by family-problems/illness/finances
- The wish-for-hastened-death in cancer tracks DEPRESSION AND PAIN, not disease-stage (the palliative finding)
- The calm-after-decision = the "finally at peace" misread; the recovered-burden-sign (pain-talk stops) = alarm
- ECT: early for the severe band, the fastest risk-depressor in geriatrics
- Connectedness architecture: fixed-ritual contacts + RETAINED roles, the protective evidence
- Means-counselling: the elder's own hoarded pharmacy counted (week-quantities, custody), pesticides locked, alone-hours mapped and filled
- The physician-opportunity: elders see doctors monthly, the two-question screen rides the vitals
- MHA 2017's care-not-custody frame applies; Tele-MANAS 14416 as the family-call spine

**Classic traps:**
1. The "old age talk" normalisation (the grammar accepted as weather for months)
2. The "finally at peace" misread of the widower's quiet
3. The "Be positive" family regime deepening the silence
4. ECT deferred to "last resort" while the elder wastes
5. The santhara-framed depression fast accepted without the mood-assessment
6. The stockpiles uncounted (the elder's own medicine-cupboard is the means)
7. The "respecting his wish" passivity in untreated depression
8. The move-to-the-children's-city transplant (loss added to illness)

# Dual Diagnosis — Psychiatric & Behavioural Disorders in Intellectual Disability

## 1. In one line + overview

**People with intellectual disability develop mental illnesses and behavioural crises at HIGHER rates than the general population, yet get diagnosed at LOWER rates, because of diagnostic overshadowing (every symptom gets explained away as "the disability itself") and because their illnesses speak through BEHAVIOUR rather than words, so the discipline of this field is a three-step law: know the person's BASELINE, audit the BODY before the mind when behaviour changes, and read behaviour as the language it is, with medication in a deliberately small, targeted, reviewed role.**

The numbers: point-prevalence of psychiatric disorders in people with ID runs roughly two-to-three times the general population's (30–40%+ by broad criteria), plus the epilepsy-and-dementia comorbidity tiers running far above base rates. And the clinical failure is systematic: the depressed man with Down whose withdrawal is "just his level"; the psychotic woman whose self-talk's escalation is "her habit"; the head-banging man whose toothache went undiagnosed for months because "he is always like that" (he was not); the sedation-as-management culture that trades a family's evening peace for the person's daytime consciousness. The two errors this field exists to correct: **diagnostic overshadowing** (the ID's gravity field that pulls every new symptom into itself) and the **behaviour-as-badness reflex** (reading the communication-channel's distress as naughtiness). The craft that corrects them: the BASELINE-documentation ("what is THIS person's normal", the family's-and-carer's testimony as the diagnostic instrument); the PHYSICAL-FIRST audit (pain, constipation, dental, ear, reflux, drugs; illness presents as behaviour in anyone whose words cannot carry it); the functional-behaviour analysis (the ABC model: what triggers, what follows, what the behaviour EARNs, because most "challenging behaviour" is the person's most effective available communication); and the psychiatry-of-ID's medication-discipline (fewer drugs, single-symptom targets, dated reviews, the antipsychotics-not-for-behaviour-alone position that the modern evidence-and-guidelines established). The Indian layer: the family (usually the mother, ageing) as the lifetime service-system; the school's-and-bus's "quiet-child" pressure that feeds the sedation-culture; and the capacity-and-consent architecture of MHA 2017 applied with the supported-decision-making frame.

## 4. Epidemiology

**Global.** The psychiatric-comorbidity rates: point-prevalence of diagnosable mental disorders in ID populations ~30–40% (broad-criteria tiers higher); the diagnosis-rate's inverse-relationship with the disability's severity (the LESS verbal the person, the MORE the illness hides; the paradox that the most dependent get the least psychiatry). The specific tiers: ADHD's high co-occurrence in mild-moderate-ID; anxiety-and-mood in the mild-band's adults at general-population-multiples; psychosis's prevalence at ~3% (roughly twice general-population, and the severe-band's presentations the most atypical); dementia's-and-epilepsy's 5–10×-tier elevations; the behavioural-"challenging-behaviour" tier: aggression-and-self-injury affecting a substantial share of the severe-profound population. The psychotropic-prescribing reality: adults with ID are prescribed antipsychotics-and-sedatives at several-fold the general-population's rates, mostly for BEHAVIOUR rather than diagnosed illness (the prescribing-culture the modern-guidelines-and-trials confronted: the antipsychotic-withdrawal-in-well-designed-studies largely maintained-behaviour, the drugs-that-had-no-diagnosis-to-treat).

**India.** No dedicated dual-diagnosis survey; the clinical reality channels through the family-and-the-GP-system: the behaviour-change-routes (the paediatric-or-the-physician-OPD's "he has become very hyper" visit; the school's suspension-letter; the temple-or-faith-route for the dramatic-onsets) and the sedation-culture's pharmacological default (the chlorpromazine-and-the-"calming-syrup"-prescriptions-issued-without-psychiatric-diagnosis, the Indian-IDIOM's quietest-scandal). The family-structure's double-edge: the joint-family's observation-density supports the baseline-knowledge (the grandmother's "this is not him" testimony is the country's best-informant-instrument), and the family's exhaustion-and-shame-tiers drive the "make-him-quiet" request that the sedation-culture-feeds. The service-gap: the ID-psychiatry-capability's metro-concentration; the district-psychiatrist's ID-caseloads without the ID-specific-discipline; the parent-organisations'-and-special-schools'-practical-knowledge-outpacing-the-clinical-tier-in-behaviour-management-craft.

## 6. What happens: the three mechanism stories

**Story 1: the gravity-well (diagnostic overshadowing).** The ID-diagnosis's-gravity-field: every-new-symptom's-observation-gets-pulled-toward "that-is-just-his-condition", the clinician's-and-the-family's-shared-cognitive-shortcut. The well's-three-faces: the SYMptom-attrition ("people-like-him-are-always-restless", the restlessness-that-was-the-hyperthyroidism-or-the-anxiety); the PRESENTATION-attrition ("she-cannot-have-depression, she-cannot-say-she-is-sad", the illness-that-needs-no-words); the TREATMENT-fatalism ("no-point-treating-psychosis-in-someone-like-him": the treatable-that-goes-untreated). The counter-instrument: the BASELINE-anchored-change-detection (the "what-changed-from-HIS-normal" question; the mother's-or-the-teacher's-testimony-as-the-differential's-spine: "he is not himself" outscores-any-rating-scale-in-this-population).

**Story 2: the body's-first-voice (illness-presents-as-behaviour).** In the person-whose-words-cannot-carry-the-inner-state, the body-and-the-behaviour-become-the-only-reporters: the toothache's-head-banging (the dental-abscess-diagnosed-after-eight-months-of-"behaviour-management"); the constipation's-aggression (the week-of-the-ungone-bowel-behind-the "unprovoked"-meltdowns); the ear-infection's-self-harm (the-hits-to-the-own-ear-read-as-the "stereotypy's-escalation"); the reflux's-night-awakening-and-the-self-injury's-post-meal-timing; the fracture-in-the-nonverbal-and-non-ambulant-changed-by-transfer-screaming; the migraine's-and-the-sinus's-and-the UTI's-irritabilities. The audit-discipline (the PHYSICAL-FIRST-checklist, the every-new-behaviour's-first-page): teeth, ears, sinuses, gut (constipation-and-reflux), urine, skin-and-nails (the ingrown-and-the-fungal-and-the-pressure-areas), bones-and-joints (the injuries-and-the-arthritis-of-the-cerebral-palsy-hips), head-and-eyes (the-visual-and-the-headache-tiers), drugs (the side-effects-and-the-additions-and-the-stops), and the cycle-and-the-menses-and-the-pregnancy-questions-where-applicable. The field's-rule-of-rules: **"new-or-worsened-behaviour-is-a-medical-symptom-until-the-body-is-cleared"**, the rule-that-catches-the-most-missed-diagnoses-in-this-population.

**Story 3: the behaviour's-job (the functional-analysis engine).** Challenging-behaviour-is-not-random: it is the person's-most-effective-available-communication, doing a JOB. The four-job-map: ATTENTION ("the only-time-anyone-slows-down-and-looks-at-me-is-when-I-scream"); ESCAPE ("this-room-this-noise-this-task-is-unbearable, the-scream-ends-it", the sensory-or-the-demand's-flight); TANGIBLE ("the-scream-gets-the-biscuit: the-only-reliable-biscuit-mechanism-I-own"); SENSORY ("the-hand-flapping-and-the-rocking-feel-regulating: the-nervous-system's-own-tuning"). The ABC-analysis's-craft: map the ANTECEDENT's-patterns (the-when-where-who-what-just-before), the BEHAVIOUR's-exact-shape, and the CONSEQUENCE's-what-the-world-then-does (the attention-and-the-removal-and-the-tangibles-that-fell); read the pattern (the 5-p.m.-meltdown's-snack-hunger-arc; the assembly-day's-escalation's-sensory-load; the new-staff-week's-testing-boundaries-pattern); and design the OPPOSITE-behaviour's-teaching (the functional-equivalent: the communication-card-that-GETS-the-break, "teach-the-replacement-that-works-as-well-as-the-scream, or-the-scream-stays"). The intervention-logic: change the ANTECEDENT-architecture (the sensory-and-the-demand-and-the-hunger-and-the-transition-engineering), teach the REPLACEMENT, and re-arrange the CONSEQUENCES (the calm-non-dramatic-response-to-the-scream; the dramatic-response-to-the-card): the-behaviour's-physics, not the-behaviour's-war.

## 9. Management

## 13. FAQ: asked the way families and carers ask it

**"His behaviour has become terrible. Isn't that just part of his condition getting worse with age?"** It is the single most expensive assumption in this field.
